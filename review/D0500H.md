The item's own pages state the staff interview is conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD (p. D-13). Counting follows the general convention of ARD plus the 6 previous calendar days (rai-manual-v1.20.1, p. 2-17: "ARD + 6 previous calendar days"). D-12) and to "select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred" (p. D-15); the frequency codes are expressed in days out of 14. ## Coding rules

1. D0500 (including item H) is not conducted if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^8]
2. The Staff Assessment of Resident Mood is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^9]
3. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^10]
4. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the resident interview is deemed not complete, the Staff Assessment of Mood is not completed, and the assessor skips to D0700.[^11]
5. The staff interviews for D0500 are conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
6. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^12]
7. If a longer item (such as D0500H, which contains two phrases) was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^13]
8. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^14]
9. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^15]
10. Encourage staff to report symptom frequency even if the staff believes the symptom to be unrelated to depression.[^16]
11. Interview staff from all shifts who know the resident best, in a location that protects resident privacy.[^17]
12. D0500H frequency scores in Column 2 are added, together with the other D0500 items, into D0600 Total Severity Score (maximum 30); the interview is successfully completed if staff answered the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items.[^18]
