## Coding rules

1. D0500H is the staff-assessed item "Moving or speaking so slowly that other people have noticed. Or the opposite - being so fidgety or restless that they have been moving around a lot more than usual," scored in Column 1 (Symptom Presence) and Column 2 (Symptom Frequency).[^8]
2. Do not conduct the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (D0500) if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^9]
3. The staff item is framed by the question: "Over the last 2 weeks, did the resident have any of the following problems or behaviors?" If the symptom is present, enter 1 (yes) in column 1, then move to column 2 and indicate symptom frequency.[^10]
4. The Staff Assessment is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^11]
5. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^12]
6. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the resident interview is deemed NOT complete; Total Severity Score is coded "99," the Staff Assessment of Mood is not completed, and the assessor skips to D0700, Social Isolation.[^13]
7. Conduct the staff interviews during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
8. Interview staff from all shifts who know the resident best; conduct the staff interview in a location that protects resident privacy.[^14]
9. Encourage staff to report symptom frequency even if the staff believes the symptom to be unrelated to depression.[^15]
10. If frequency cannot be determined by staff interview because the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, talk to family or significant other and review transfer records to inform the selection of a frequency code.[^16]
11. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^17]
12. If a longer item (such as D0500H, which contains two parts) was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^18]
13. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^19]
14. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^20]
15. The staff interview is successfully completed if staff members were able to answer the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items on the PHQ-9-OV©; frequency scores from all D0500 Column 2 items are summed for D0600 (maximum 30).[^21]

## What the chart must show

1. When the resident has been in the facility less than 2 weeks and staff cannot determine frequency, information from family/significant other and review of transfer records supports the frequency code selected. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^16]
