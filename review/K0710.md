## Coding rules

1. Complete K0710 only if Column 2 and/or Column 3 are checked for K0520A and/or K0520B.[^7]
2. K0710 is coded in two columns: Column 2, While a Resident (performed while a resident of this facility and within the last 7 days), and Column 3, During Entire 7 Days (performed during the entire last 7 days).[^8]
3. For K0710A, review intake records within the last 7 days to determine actual intake through parenteral or tube feeding routes, then calculate the proportion of total calories received through these routes.[^9]
4. For K0710A, if the resident took no food or fluids by mouth or took just sips of fluid, stop and code 3, 51% or more.[^10]
5. For K0710A, if the resident had more substantial oral intake than sips of fluid, consult with the qualified dietitian or other clinically qualified nutrition professional.[^11]
6. For K0710A, select the best response among 1 (25% or less), 2 (26% to 50%), and 3 (51% or more).[^12]
7. For K0710B, review intake records from the last 7 days and add up the total amount of fluid received each day by IV and/or tube feedings only.[^13]
8. For K0710B, divide the week's total fluid intake by 7 to calculate the average fluid intake per day; divide by 7 even if the resident did not receive IV fluids and/or tube feeding on each of the 7 days.[^14]
9. Code K0710B for the average number of cc per day of fluid the resident received via IV or tube feeding; record what was actually received by the resident, not what was ordered.[^15]
10. For Column 3 (During Entire 7 Days), fluid intake includes fluid received while in the hospital as well as while a resident of the nursing home, and the total is divided by 7 days; for Column 2 (While a Resident), only fluid received while a resident is counted and averaged over the days as a resident (example divides 1,520 cc by 3 days).[^16]
