## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

O0110 uses a different assessment period for each column. Column b (While a Resident) uses the 14-day window — treatments performed while a resident of this facility and within the last 14 days — and is recorded here as the lookback. Column a (On Admission) uses days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B, and column c (At Discharge) uses the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C (p. O-1, p. O-3). The document does not state how the 14 days are counted relative to the ARD for this item.

## Coding rules

1. Code only tracheal and/or nasopharyngeal suctioning in O0110D1; do not code oral suctioning here.[^2]
2. O0110D1 may be coded when the resident performs their own tracheal and/or nasopharyngeal suctioning.[^5]
3. Check O0110D2 (Scheduled) when suctioning was scheduled; scheduled suctioning applies to medical orders for performing suctioning at specific intervals and/or implementation of facility-based clinical standards, protocols, and guidelines.[^6]
4. Check O0110D3 (As needed) when suctioning was performed on an as-needed basis rather than at regular scheduled intervals (e.g., secretions so prominent that gurgling or choking is noted, or a sudden desaturation from a mucus plug).[^4]
5. Column a (On Admission): check the treatment if it was received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident on days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; if no treatments, procedures, or programs occurred in that 3-day period, check Z, None of the above.[^7]
6. Column b (While a Resident): check the treatment if the resident received or performed it after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days; if none occurred within the last 14 days or since admission/entry or reentry, check Z, None of the above.[^8]
7. Column c (At Discharge): check the treatment if it was received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C; if none occurred in that 3-day period, check Z, None of the above.[^9]
8. Facilities may code treatments, programs, and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^10]
9. Do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^11]
10. In the O0110 item matrix, D1 (Suctioning) has check boxes under all three columns (a. On Admission, b. While a Resident, c. At Discharge), while the sub-items D2 (Scheduled) and D3 (As needed) have check boxes only under column a and column c.[^12]
