## Coding rules

1. Add scores for questions C0200–C0400 and fill in the total score (00–15); enter 99 if the resident was unable to complete the interview.[^4]
2. After completing C0200–C0400, add up the values for all questions from C0200 through C0400; do not add up the score while interviewing the resident.[^5]
3. If the resident chooses not to answer a specific question(s), that question is coded as incorrect and the item(s) counts in the total score; if the resident chooses not to answer four or more items, the interview is coded as incomplete and the Staff Assessment for Mental Status is completed.[^6]
4. To be considered a completed interview, the resident had to attempt and provide relevant answers to at least four of the questions included in C0200–C0400; a relevant response only has to be related to the question (logical), it does not have to be correct.[^7]
5. A 0 score does not mean the BIMS was incomplete; for the BIMS to be incomplete a resident must choose not to answer or must give completely unrelated, nonsensical responses to four or more items. If one or more of the 0s in C0200–C0300 are due to incorrect answers, the interview should continue.[^8]
6. If a resident can communicate but chooses not to participate in the BIMS and does not attempt any items, this is an incomplete interview: enter 99 for C0500 and complete the Staff Assessment for Mental Status.[^9]
7. If all of the BIMS items are coded with a dash, C0500 must also be coded with a dash.[^3]
8. If the BIMS interview is stopped before completion, code 99 in the BIMS Summary Score (C0500); if the assessment is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge, continue to C1310, otherwise code 1, yes in C0600 and complete the Staff Assessment for Mental Status.[^10]
9. When the BIMS is completed and scored between 00 and 15, C0600 is coded 0, no and the Staff Assessment for Mental Status is not completed; 00 is a legitimate value for C0500 indicating a complete interview.[^11]
10. When C0600 is coded 1, yes (resident chose not to participate or four or more items coded 0 for refusal/nonsensical response), C0500 should be coded 99.[^12]
11. If the resident interview was not conducted within the look-back period (preferably the day before or the day of) the ARD, C0100 must be coded 1, Yes, and the standard "no information" code (a dash "-") entered in the resident interview items.[^13]
