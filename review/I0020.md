## Coding rules

1. Item completion gate as printed in the item box: complete only if A0310B = 01, or if the state requires completion with an OBRA assessment.[^15]
2. Item completion gate as printed in the Coding Instructions: complete only if A0310B = 01 or 08.[^16]
3. Indicate the primary medical condition category that best describes the primary reason for the Medicare Part A stay, then proceed to I0020B and enter the ICD code for that condition, including the decimal.[^17]
4. An acute condition that is the primary reason for the SNF stay may be coded in I0020B; however, because residents more commonly present for care related to an aftereffect of a disease, condition, or injury, subsequent encounter or sequelae codes should be used.[^18]
5. The primary medical condition coded in I0020B must also be included in items I0100–I8000: Active Diagnoses in the Last 7 Days.[^19]
6. If hip replacement is secondary to hip fracture, do not code 09 Hip and Knee Replacement; code as fracture (10, Fractures and Other Multiple Trauma).[^20]
7. Where a resident is admitted for rehabilitation following a total hip replacement performed because of a hip fracture, the primary medical condition category is 10, Fractures and Other Multiple Trauma (not hip/knee replacement).[^21]
8. Surgical placement of a central line does not change the resident's care to a surgical category because it is not considered to be a major surgery (example rationale supporting coding 13, Medically Complex Conditions).[^22]
