## Coding rules

1. Code peritoneal or renal dialysis that occurs at the nursing home or at another facility; record treatments of hemofiltration, Slow Continuous Ultrafiltration (SCUF), Continuous Arteriovenous Hemofiltration (CAVH), and Continuous Ambulatory Peritoneal Dialysis (CAPD) in this item.[^6]
2. IVs, IV medication, and blood transfusions administered during dialysis are considered part of the dialysis procedure and are not to be coded under K0520A (Parenteral/IV), O0110H (IV medications), or O0110I (transfusions).[^7]
3. This item may be coded if the resident performs their own dialysis.[^8]
4. Check O0110J2, Hemodialysis, when the dialysis was hemodialysis (blood is circulated directly through a dialysis machine that uses special filters to remove waste products and excess fluid from the blood).[^9]
5. Check O0110J3, Peritoneal dialysis, when the dialysis was peritoneal dialysis (dialysate is infused into the peritoneal cavity and the peritoneum serves as a filter to remove waste products and excess fluid from the blood).[^10]
6. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff; do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical or diagnostic procedure (surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures).[^11]
7. Column a (On Admission): check dialysis received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident on days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; if none in the 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^12]
8. Column b (While a Resident): check dialysis the resident received or performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days; if none within the last 14 days or since admission/entry or reentry, check Z, None of the above.[^13]
9. Column c (At Discharge): check dialysis received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C; if none in the 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^14]
