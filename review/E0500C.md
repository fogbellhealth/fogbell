## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The item's own Steps for Assessment (p. E-8) state a 7-day look-back period for the behaviors identified in E0200 that are considered when coding E0500A-C. Per the general convention, the ARD is the last day of the look-back period and the window is the ARD plus the 6 previous calendar days (rai-manual-v1.20.1, p. 2-21).

## Coding rules

1. E0500C is completed only when E0300 is coded 1 (yes) — i.e., when any of E0200A, E0200B, or E0200C were coded 1, 2, or 3; if E0300 is coded 0, skip to E0800.[^4]
2. Code E0500A, E0500B, and E0500C based on all of the behavioral symptoms coded in E0200.[^5]
3. Determine whether the behaviors significantly interfered with the resident's participation in activities or social interactions.[^6]
4. Code 0, no, if none of the identified symptom(s) significantly interfered with the resident's participation in activities or social interactions.[^2]
5. Code 1, yes, if any of the identified behavioral symptom(s) significantly interfered with or decreased the resident's participation, or caused staff not to include residents in activities or social interactions.[^3]
6. For E0500C, code if the impact of the resident's behavior is limiting or keeping the resident from engaging in solitary activities or hobbies, joining groups, or attending programmed activities or having positive social encounters with visitors, other residents, or staff.[^7]
7. Resident intent must not be taken into account when coding items in Section E.[^8]

## What the chart must show

1. Review of the medical record for the 7-day look-back period supporting the behaviors identified in E0200 and their impact. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^1]
   Listed as Step 1 of the Steps for Assessment for E0500.
2. Staff interviews across all shifts and disciplines, plus interviews with others who had close interactions with the resident, and direct observations of the behaviors identified in E0200. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^1]
3. Documentation that supports comparison of subsequent assessments to a baseline to identify changes in the resident's behavior, including response to interventions. *(importance to an auditor: low)*[^9]
   Framed as care-planning rationale rather than a required coding document.
