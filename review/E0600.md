## Coding rules

1. E0600 is completed only when E0300 is coded 1, yes (any of E0200A, E0200B, or E0200C coded 1, 2, or 3); if E0300 is coded 0, skip to E0800.[^8]
2. To code E0600, determine if the behaviors identified (in E0200) put others at significant risk of physical illness or injury, intruded on their privacy or activities, and/or interfered with their care or living environments.[^9]
3. E0600A: code 0 if none of the identified behavioral symptoms placed staff, visitors, or other residents at significant risk for physical injury; code 1 if any did.[^3]
4. E0600B: code 1 if any identified behavioral symptom kept other residents from enjoying privacy or engaging in informal activities (not organized or run by staff), including coming in uninvited, invading, or forcing oneself on others' private activities.[^5]
5. E0600C: code 1 if any identified behavioral symptom created a climate of excessive noise or interfered with the receipt of care or participation in organized activities by other residents.[^7]
6. For E0600A, code based on whether the behavior placed others at significant risk for physical injury; physical injury is trauma that results in pain or other distressing physical symptoms, impaired organ function, physical disability or other adverse consequences, regardless of the need for medical, surgical, nursing, or rehabilitative intervention.[^10]
7. For E0600B, code based on whether the behavior violates other residents' privacy or interrupts other residents' ADL performance or limits engagement in/enjoyment of informal social or recreational activities to such an extent that it causes the other residents distress or inconvenience, whether or not the other residents complain.[^11]
8. For E0600C, code based on whether the behavior interferes with staff ability to deliver care or conduct organized activities, interrupts receipt of care or participation in organized activities by other residents, and/or causes other residents to experience distress or adverse consequences.[^12]
9. E0600 is coded based on the behaviors identified in item E0200 and their impact on others.[^13]
