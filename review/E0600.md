## Coding rules

1. E0600 is completed only when E0300 is coded 1, yes (any of E0200A, E0200B, or E0200C coded 1, 2, or 3); if E0300 is coded 0, skip to E0800.[^8]
2. To code E0600, determine if the behaviors identified (in E0200) put others at significant risk of physical illness or injury, intruded on their privacy or activities, and/or interfered with their care or living environments.[^9]
3. For E0600A, code based on whether the behavior placed others at significant risk for physical injury; physical injury is trauma resulting in pain or other distressing physical symptoms, impaired organ function, physical disability or other adverse consequences, regardless of the need for medical, surgical, nursing, or rehabilitative intervention.[^10]
4. For E0600B, code based on whether the behavior violates other residents' privacy or interrupts other residents' ADL performance or limits engagement in or enjoyment of informal social or recreational activities to such an extent that it causes the other residents distress or inconvenience — whether or not the other residents complain.[^11]
5. For E0600C, code based on whether the behavior interferes with staff ability to deliver care or conduct organized activities, interrupts receipt of care or participation in organized activities by other residents, and/or causes other residents to experience distress or adverse consequences.[^12]
6. Intrusion on another resident's privacy is coded 1 at E0600B regardless of whether the affected residents complain or communicate distress (example: repeatedly entering others' rooms and rummaging through belongings).[^13]
7. Resident intent is not taken into account when coding items in Section E; coding focuses on the resident's actions rather than the intent of their behavior.[^14]

## What the chart must show

1. Review of the clinical record for the 7-day look-back period, considered together with the prior review performed for the behaviors identified in E0200. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^1]
   Listed as step 1 of Steps for Assessment for E0600.
2. Staff interviews across all shifts and disciplines, plus interviews with others who had close interactions with the resident, regarding the behaviors identified in E0200. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^15]
3. Observations of the behaviors identified in E0200 during the 7-day look-back period. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^16]
4. Documentation supporting comparison of subsequent assessments to a baseline to identify changes in the resident's behavior, including response to interventions. *(importance to an auditor: low)*[^17]
   Framed as care-planning rationale, not as a coding requirement.
