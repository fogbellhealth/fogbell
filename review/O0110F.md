## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

O0110 uses column-specific assessment periods. Column b (While a Resident) — the general observation window — is the last 14 days after admission/entry or reentry (p. O-3); Item Rationale also refers to "the 14-day look-back period" (p. O-2). Column a (On Admission) uses days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B, and column c (At Discharge) uses the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C (p. O-3). Separately, the F1 coding tip references residents weaned off a respirator/ventilator "in the last 14 days" (p. O-5). The document does not state that the window is counted as ARD plus prior days for this item.

## Coding rules

1. Code in O0110F1 any type of electrically or pneumatically powered closed-system mechanical ventilator support device that ensures adequate ventilation in a resident who is, or may become (such as during weaning attempts), unable to support their own respiration.[^5]
2. During invasive mechanical ventilation the resident's breathing is controlled by the ventilator; residents receiving closed-system ventilation include those receiving ventilation via an endotracheal tube (nasally or orally intubated) or tracheostomy.[^6]
3. A resident who has been weaned off a respirator or ventilator in the last 14 days, or who is currently being weaned off a respirator or ventilator, should also be coded here.[^7]
4. Do not code O0110F1 when the ventilator or respirator is used only as a substitute for BiPAP or CPAP.[^8]
5. When a ventilator or respirator is being used as a substitute for BiPAP/CPAP, it is coded in O0110G1 (Non-invasive Mechanical Ventilator) instead.[^9]
6. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff; do not code services provided solely in conjunction with a surgical or diagnostic procedure, such as IV medications or ventilators (surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures).[^10]
7. If no treatments, procedures, or programs were received or performed in a column's assessment period, check Z, None of the above for that column.[^11]
8. An invasive mechanical ventilator delivering an FiO2 exceeding 40% may also be counted as a high-concentration oxygen delivery system for O0110C4.[^12]
