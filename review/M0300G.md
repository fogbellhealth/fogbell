## Coding rules

1. M0300G1: Enter the number of unstageable pressure injuries related to deep tissue injury. Based on skin tone, the injured tissue area may present as a darker tone than the surrounding intact skin; these areas of discoloration are potentially areas of deep tissue injury.[^3]
2. M0300G1: Enter 0 if no unstageable pressure injuries related to deep tissue injury are present, and skip to M1030, Number of Venous and Arterial Ulcers.[^4]
3. M0300G2: Enter the number of these unstageable pressure injuries related to deep tissue injury that were first noted at the time of admission/entry AND—for residents reentering the facility after a hospital stay—that were acquired during the hospitalization (i.e., not acquired in the nursing facility prior to admission to the hospital).[^5]
4. M0300G2: Enter 0 if no unstageable pressure injuries related to deep tissue injury were first noted at the time of admission/entry or reentry.[^6]
5. For purposes of coding, the lesion assessed must be primarily a result of pressure with other conditions ruled out; if pressure is not the primary cause, do not code here.[^7]
6. If the tissue adjacent to, or surrounding, an intact blister does not show signs of tissue damage (e.g., color change, tenderness, bogginess or firmness, warmth or coolness), do not code as a deep tissue injury.[^8]
7. When a lesion due to pressure presents with an intact blister AND the surrounding or adjacent soft tissue does NOT have the characteristics of deep tissue injury, do not code in M0300G (see Stage 2 pressure ulcer definition).[^9]
8. Once a deep tissue injury has opened to an ulcer, reclassify the ulcer into the appropriate stage and code the ulcer for the reclassified stage (i.e., no longer at M0300G).[^10]
9. A pressure injury with intact skin that is a deep tissue injury (DTI) should not be coded as a Stage 1 pressure injury; it should be coded as unstageable.[^11]
10. Present-on-admission determination (referenced by M0300G Step 6): if a pressure ulcer/injury was unstageable on admission/entry or reentry and then becomes numerically stageable later, it is considered "present on admission" at the stage at which it first becomes numerically stageable; if it subsequently increases in numerical stage, that higher stage is not coded as present on admission.[^12]
11. Present-on-admission determination: if a pressure ulcer/injury was unstageable on admission/entry or reentry and then becomes unstageable for another reason, it should be considered "present on admission" at the new unstageable status (e.g., admitted with a deep tissue injury that later opens and is covered with slough while remaining unstageable).[^13]
12. For each pressure ulcer/injury, present on admission means present at the time of admission/entry or reentry and not acquired while the resident was in the care of the nursing home; consider current and historical levels of tissue involvement.[^14]

## What the chart must show

1. Assessment findings for the deep tissue injury are clearly documented in the resident's medical record, with tracking and documentation of appropriate wound care planning and management. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^15]
   Stated as an assessment step directive in the M0300G Steps for Assessment.
2. A head-to-toe/full body skin assessment focusing on bony prominences and pressure-bearing areas (sacrum, buttocks, heels, ankles, etc.) supports identification of DTIs coded here. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^16]
   Framed as a step for assessment rather than an explicit documentation requirement.
3. Evidence that the area adjacent to or surrounding an intact blister was examined for signs of tissue damage (color change, tenderness, bogginess or firmness, warmth or coolness) to distinguish DTI from a Stage 2 pressure ulcer. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^17]
4. Documentation supporting the present-on-admission determination, including review of the medical record for the history of the ulcer/injury and its location and stage at the time of admission/entry or reentry. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^18]
   From the Step 3 "Determine Present on Admission" process referenced by M0300G Step 6.
