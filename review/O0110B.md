## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

The observation window is column-specific. Column b (While a Resident) uses the last 14 days (after admission/entry or reentry); the Item Rationale on p. O-2 also refers to "the 14-day look-back period." Two other stated windows apply to the other columns: Column a (On Admission) = days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B, and Column c (At Discharge) = the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C (p. O-3). The manual does not state how the 14 days are counted relative to the ARD.

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. Code intermittent radiation therapy, as well as radiation administered via radiation implant, in item O0110B1.[^4]
2. Radiation (B1) is available for checking in all three columns of O0110: a. On Admission, b. While a Resident, and c. At Discharge.[^5]
3. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^6]
4. Do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^7]
5. Column a: check if radiation was received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident on days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; if no treatments, procedures, or programs were received or performed in the 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^8]
6. Column b: check if radiation was received or performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days; if none within the last 14 days or since admission/entry or reentry, check Z, None of the above.[^9]
7. Column c: check if radiation was received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C; if none in the 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^10]
8. Determination is made by reviewing the medical record for receipt/performance of the treatment within the assessment period defined for each column.[^11]

## What the chart must show

1. The resident's medical record must show that radiation therapy was received or performed within the assessment period defined for the column being coded. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^11]

## ⚠️ Conflicts — please read carefully

> **Coexistence of multiple observation windows for O0110B (14 days vs. 3 days)**
>
> - *Federal manual:* Column b (While a Resident) uses a 14-day window: treatments received or performed after admission/entry or reentry and within the last 14 days.[^1]
> - *Federal manual:* Columns a and c use 3-day windows: days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay (A2400B) and the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay (A2400C).[^12]
>
> Status: Resolved by the document: the window is column-specific — "Review the resident’s medical record to determine whether or not the resident received or performed any of the treatments, procedures, or programs within the assessment period defined for each column" (p. O-3). The 14-day window applies to column b; the 3-day windows apply to columns a and c.
