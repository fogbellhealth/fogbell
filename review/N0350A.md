## Coding rules

1. Enter in Item N0350A the number of days during the 7-day look-back period (or since admission/entry or reentry if less than 7 days) that insulin injections were received.[^1]
2. For subcutaneous insulin pumps, code only the number of days that the resident actually required a subcutaneous injection to restart the pump. (Stated in the Coding Tips and Special Populations section of N0350, which appears after the Coding Instructions for N0350B; the tip is not explicitly limited to a single sub-item.)[^3]
3. Insulin injections are counted in Item N0350 as well as in Item N0300 (Injections).[^4]

## What the chart must show

1. Resident's medication administration records for the 7-day look-back period (or since admission/entry or reentry if less than 7 days) documenting the days insulin injections were received. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^5]
   Listed as step 1 of the Steps for Assessment for N0350.
2. Documentation establishing that the resident received insulin injections during the look-back period. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^6]
   Steps for Assessment item 2.
3. A count of the number of days insulin injections were received (and/or insulin orders changed) during the look-back period. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^7]
   Steps for Assessment item 4; covers both N0350A and N0350B.
