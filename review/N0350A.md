## Coding rules

1. Enter in Item N0350A the number of days during the 7-day look-back period (or since admission/entry or reentry if less than 7 days) that insulin injections were received.[^1]
2. For subcutaneous insulin pumps, code only the number of days that the resident actually required a subcutaneous injection to restart the pump.[^3]
3. Insulin injections are counted in this item (N0350) as well as in Item N0300, Injections.[^4]

## What the chart must show
