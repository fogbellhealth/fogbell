## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

The assessor observation window differs by column. Column b (While a Resident) uses the last 14 days after admission/entry or reentry — recorded here as the look-back window = 14. Column a (On Admission) uses days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B (p. O-3, p. O-1). Column c (At Discharge) uses the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C (p. O-3, p. O-1). Note that the printed item table on p. O-1 provides column b check boxes only for G1; G2 (BiPAP) and G3 (CPAP) have check boxes only in columns a and c.

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. Code any type of CPAP or BiPAP respiratory support device that prevents airways from closing by delivering slightly pressurized air through a mask or other device continuously or via electronic cycling throughout the breathing cycle.[^7]
2. If a ventilator or respirator is being used as a substitute for BiPAP/CPAP, code it here (in O0110G1) rather than as an invasive mechanical ventilator.[^8]
3. This item may be coded if the resident places or removes their own BiPAP/CPAP mask/device.[^9]
4. O0110G2, BiPAP: check if the non-invasive mechanical ventilator support was BiPAP.[^5]
5. O0110G3, CPAP: check if the non-invasive mechanical ventilator support was CPAP.[^6]
6. Column a (On Admission): check the item if it was received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident on days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; if nothing was received or performed in that 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^10]
7. Column b (While a Resident): check the item if it was received or performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days; if nothing was received or performed within the last 14 days or since admission/entry or reentry, check Z, None of the above.[^1]
8. Column c (At Discharge): check the item if it was received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C; if nothing was received or performed in that 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^11]
9. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^12]
10. Do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure, such as IV medications or ventilators; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^13]
11. Do not code O0110F1 (Invasive Mechanical Ventilator) when the ventilator or respirator is used only as a substitute for BiPAP or CPAP (such use is coded under O0110G1 instead).[^14]
