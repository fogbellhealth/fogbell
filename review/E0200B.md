## Coding rules

1. E0200B captures verbal behavioral symptoms directed toward others (e.g., threatening others, screaming at others, cursing at others); enter a code in the box.[^6]
2. Code based on whether the symptoms occurred, not on an interpretation of the behavior's meaning or cause or the assessor's judgment that the behavior can be explained or should be tolerated.[^7]
3. Code as present even if staff have become used to the behavior or view it as typical or tolerable.[^8]
4. Behaviors in these categories are coded as present or not present whether or not they might represent a rejection of care.[^9]
5. Resident intent must not be taken into account when coding items in Section E; the section focuses on the resident's actions, not the intent of the behavior.[^10]
6. The coding of E0200B feeds item E0300: if E0200A, E0200B, and E0200C are all coded 0, E0300 is coded 0 (no) and the assessor skips to E0800; if any of E0200A, E0200B, or E0200C is coded 1, 2, or 3, E0300 is coded 1 (yes) and E0500 and E0600 are completed.[^11]
7. All behavioral symptoms coded in E0200 (including E0200B) are the basis for coding the impact items E0500A–C and E0600A–C.[^12]
