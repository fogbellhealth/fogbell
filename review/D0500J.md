## Coding rules

1. D0500J is the tenth symptom item of the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (PHQ-9-OV©): "Being short-tempered, easily annoyed," coded in two columns — 1. Symptom Presence and 2. Symptom Frequency.[^8]
2. Do not conduct the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (D0500, including J) if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^9]
3. The staff assessment asks, for each listed problem or behavior including J, whether the resident had it over the last 2 weeks; if the symptom is present, enter 1 (yes) in column 1, Symptom Presence, then move to column 2, Symptom Frequency, and indicate symptom frequency.[^10]
4. Conduct the staff interviews during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
5. Interview staff from all shifts who know the resident best; conduct the staff interview in a location that protects resident privacy.[^11]
6. If frequency cannot be determined by staff interview because the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, talk to family or significant other and review transfer records to inform the selection of a frequency code.[^12]
7. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^13]
8. If a longer item was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^14]
9. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^15]
10. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^16]
11. The Staff Assessment (D0500) is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^17]
12. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^18]
13. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the resident interview is deemed NOT complete, D0160 is coded 99, the Staff Assessment of Mood is not completed, and the assessor skips to D0700, Social Isolation.[^19]
14. Frequency responses coded in Column 2 for D0500 items (including J) are summed into D0600, Total Severity Score; the maximum score is 30 (3 × 10) and the interview is successfully completed if staff answered the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items on the PHQ-9-OV©.[^20]

## ⚠️ Conflicts — please read carefully

> **Observation window for D0500 staff assessment: 7-day look-back vs. 2-week symptom recall**
>
> - *Federal manual:* The staff interviews are conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
> - *Federal manual:* The item stem and coding tips reference a 2-week recall period, with frequency categories spanning 0–14 days.[^23]
> - *Federal manual:* Coding tips instruct the assessor to have staff select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred.[^24]
>
> Status: unresolved — surface to user. The 7-day period is the window during which the interview is conducted (assessor look-back); the 2-week period is the symptom-recall window inside the questions. Both are recorded.
