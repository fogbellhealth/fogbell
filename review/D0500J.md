## Coding rules

1. D0500J is item J of the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (PHQ-9-OV©): "Being short-tempered, easily annoyed," coded in two columns (1. Symptom Presence and 2. Symptom Frequency).[^8]
2. Do not conduct the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (D0500, including J) if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^9]
3. The staff assessment asks whether the resident had the problem or behavior over the last 2 weeks; if the symptom is present enter 1 (yes) in Column 1, Symptom Presence, then move to Column 2, Symptom Frequency, and indicate symptom frequency.[^10]
4. The Staff Assessment is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^11]
5. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^12]
6. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the resident interview is deemed not complete, D0160 is coded "99," the Staff Assessment of Mood is not completed, and the assessor skips to D0700, Social Isolation.[^13]
7. Conduct the staff interviews during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD, interviewing staff from all shifts who know the resident best.[^1]
8. Encourage staff to report symptom frequency even if the staff believes the symptom to be unrelated to depression.[^14]
9. If frequency cannot be determined by staff interview because the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, talk to family or significant other and review transfer records to inform the selection of a frequency code.[^15]
10. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^16]
11. If a longer item was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^17]
12. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^18]
13. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^19]
14. D0500J's frequency score in Column 2 is one of the 10 items added into D0600, Total Severity Score (maximum 30); the staff interview is successfully completed if staff answered the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items.[^20]
