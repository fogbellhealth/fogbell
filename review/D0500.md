## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The item's own pages state the staff interviews are conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD (p. D-13). Note that the item itself asks staff about symptoms "Over the last 2 weeks" (item display, p. D-12; coding tips, p. D-15), i.e., the symptom-recall period inside the staff interview questions is 2 weeks (14 days) while the assessor's interview-conduct window is the 7-day look-back period. Per the general convention (p. 2-21), a 7-day observation period consists of the ARD plus the 6 previous calendar days.

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. Do not conduct the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (D0500) if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^18]
2. The staff assessment asks, for each listed problem or behavior, whether the resident had it over the last 2 weeks; if the symptom is present, enter 1 (yes) in column 1, Symptom Presence, then move to column 2, Symptom Frequency, and indicate symptom frequency.[^19]
3. Conduct the staff interviews during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
4. Interview staff from all shifts who know the resident best; conduct the staff interview in a location that protects resident privacy.[^20]
5. If frequency cannot be determined by staff interview because the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, talk to family or significant other and review transfer records to inform the selection of a frequency code.[^21]
6. Code 0 (no) in Column 1 if the symptoms listed are not present, and enter 0 in Column 2, Symptom Frequency.[^2]
7. Code 1 (yes) in Column 1 if the symptoms listed are present, and enter 0, 1, 2, or 3 in Column 2, Symptom Frequency.[^3]
8. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^22]
9. If a longer item was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^23]
10. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^24]
11. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^25]
12. The Staff Assessment (D0500) is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^26]
13. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^27]
14. When D0100 = 0 (resident rarely/never understood, cannot respond, or an interpreter is needed but not available), skip to item D0500, Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (PHQ-9-OV©), unless the assessment is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge, in which case skip to D0700, Social Isolation.[^28]
15. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the interview is deemed NOT complete, Total Severity Score is coded "99," the Staff Assessment of Mood is not completed, and the assessor skips to D0700, Social Isolation.[^29]
16. The Staff Assessment interview is successfully completed if the staff members were able to answer the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items on the PHQ-9-OV© (stated under D0600, Total Severity Score).[^30]
