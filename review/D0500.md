## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The manual instructs that the staff interviews be conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD (p. D-13). Note that the symptom frequency questions themselves ask about the last 2 weeks ("Over the last 2 weeks, did the resident have any of the following problems or behaviors?", p. D-12); the manual does not further explain how the 7-day interview window and the 2-week symptom reference period interact.

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. Do not conduct D0500 if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^8]
2. The item asks, for each of the 10 symptoms (A–J), whether over the last 2 weeks the resident had the problem or behavior; if the symptom is present, enter 1 (yes) in column 1, Symptom Presence, then move to column 2, Symptom Frequency, and indicate symptom frequency.[^9]
3. Conduct the staff interviews during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
4. Interview staff from all shifts who know the resident best; conduct the staff interview in a location that protects resident privacy.[^10]
5. Encourage staff to report symptom frequency, even if the staff believes the symptom to be unrelated to depression.[^11]
6. If frequency cannot be determined by staff interview because the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, talk to family or significant other and review transfer records to inform the selection of a frequency code.[^12]
7. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^13]
8. If a longer item was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^14]
9. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^15]
10. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^16]
11. The Staff Assessment (D0500) is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^17]
12. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^18]
13. When D0100 = 0 (interview should not be conducted), skip to item D0500, Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (PHQ-9-OV©), unless the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge; in that case skip to D0700, Social Isolation.[^19]
14. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the resident interview is deemed NOT complete, Total Severity Score is coded "99," the Staff Assessment of Mood is not completed, and the assessor skips to D0700, Social Isolation.[^20]
15. The staff interview is successfully completed if the staff members were able to answer the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items on the PHQ-9-OV© (criterion stated under D0600, which totals D0500 Column 2).[^21]
