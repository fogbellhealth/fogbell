## Coding rules

1. Code residents identified as being in a hospice program for terminally ill persons where an array of services is provided for the palliation and management of terminal illness and related conditions.[^3]
2. The hospice must be licensed by the state as a hospice provider and/or certified under the Medicare program as a hospice provider.[^4]
3. Column b (While a Resident): check all treatments, procedures, and programs the resident received or performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days.[^1]
4. If nothing in O0110 was received, performed or participated in within the last 14 days or since admission/entry or reentry, check Z, None of the above.[^2]
5. O0110Z1, None of the above: code if none of the listed treatments, procedures, or programs were received or performed by the resident.[^5]
6. General O0110 rule: facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^6]
7. General O0110 rule: do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure, such as IV medications or ventilators; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^7]
8. On the O0110 item matrix, K1 Hospice care is offered with a check box only under column b, While a Resident (no check box under column a, On Admission, or column c, At Discharge).[^8]
