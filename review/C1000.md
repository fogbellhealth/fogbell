## Coding rules

1. Record the resident's actual performance in making everyday decisions about tasks or activities of daily living; enter one number that corresponds to the most correct response.[^5]
2. Code what the resident is actually doing (performance), not whether staff believe the resident might be capable of doing so.[^6]
3. Where a staff member takes decision-making responsibility away from the resident regarding tasks of everyday living, or the resident does not participate in decision making, whatever their level of capability, code the resident as impaired performance in decision making.[^7]
4. If the resident "rarely or never" made decisions despite being provided with opportunities and appropriate cues, code 3, severely impaired; if the resident makes decisions, although poorly, code 2, moderately impaired.[^8]
5. A resident's considered decision to exercise their right to decline treatment or recommendations by interdisciplinary team members must not be captured as impaired decision making in C1000.[^9]
6. C1000 is part of the Staff Assessment for Mental Status (C0700–C1000), which is not conducted if the Brief Interview for Mental Status (C0200–C0500) was completed.[^10]
7. The Staff Assessment for Mental Status (beginning at C0700 and including C1000) is performed when C0600 is coded 1, yes (resident chose not to participate in the BIMS or four or more items were coded 0 due to no answer or nonsensical response).[^11]
