## Coding rules

1. Complete K0710 (including K0710A) only if Column 2 and/or Column 3 are checked for K0520A and/or K0520B.[^5]
2. Review intake records within the last 7 days to determine actual intake through parenteral or tube feeding routes, then calculate the proportion of total calories received through these routes.[^6]
3. If the resident took no food or fluids by mouth or took just sips of fluid, stop and code 3, 51% or more.[^7]
4. If the resident had more substantial oral intake than sips of fluid, consult with the qualified dietitian or other clinically qualified nutrition professional.[^8]
5. Select the best response among 1 (25% or less), 2 (26% to 50%), and 3 (51% or more).[^9]
6. Column 2 captures intake performed while a resident of this facility and within the last 7 days; Column 3 captures intake performed during the entire last 7 days.[^10]
7. Compute the percentage as total calories received by tube/parenteral route divided by total calories (oral plus tube) received over the 7 days, and code the resulting percentage band (example: 15,000/17,450 = 85.9% → code 3).[^11]
