## Coding rules

1. Code E0600C 0 (no) if none of the identified behavioral symptoms significantly disrupted delivery of care or the living environment.[^2]
2. Code E0600C 1 (yes) if any of the identified behavioral symptoms created a climate of excessive noise or interfered with the receipt of care or participation in organized activities by other residents.[^3]
3. For E0600C, code based on whether the behavior interferes with staff ability to deliver care or conduct organized activities, interrupts receipt of care or participation in organized activities by other residents, and/or causes other residents to experience distress or adverse consequences.[^4]
4. E0600 (including E0600C) is coded based on the behaviors identified in E0200; determine whether those behaviors interfered with others' care or living environments.[^5]
5. E0600 is completed only when E0300 is coded 1, yes (any of E0200A, E0200B, or E0200C coded 1, 2, or 3); if E0300 is coded 0, skip to E0800.[^6]
6. Resident intent is not taken into account when coding items in Section E; coding focuses on the resident's actions.[^7]

## What the chart must show

1. Review of the clinical record for the 7-day look-back period supporting the identified behavioral symptoms and their impact. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^1]
   Stated as step 1 of Steps for Assessment for E0600.
2. Staff interviews across all shifts and disciplines, and interviews with others who had close interactions with the resident, plus observations of the E0200 behaviors. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^1]
3. Documentation supporting a determination that the behavior disrupted care or the living environment (e.g., excessive noise, interference with staff delivery of care or organized activities, distress or adverse consequences for other residents). *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^4]
   Framed as a coding tip rather than an explicit documentation requirement.
4. Subsequent assessments and documentation can be compared with a baseline to identify changes in the resident's behavior, including response to interventions. *(importance to an auditor: low)*[^8]
   Stated under Planning for Care, not as a coding requirement.
