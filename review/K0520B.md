## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

Column 2 and Column 3 use a 7-day look-back period (Column 2 = performed prior to admission/entry or reentry and within the last 7 days; Column 3 = performed after admission/entry or reentry and within the last 7 days). Other windows apply to the other columns: Column 1 covers the first 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay (item table: "Assessment period is days 1 through 3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B") and Column 4 covers the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ("Assessment period is the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending on A2400C"). The manual does not state how the 7-day window is counted relative to the ARD for this item.

## Coding rules

1. K0520B is the feeding tube item — nasogastric or abdominal (PEG); check all that apply across the applicable columns.[^6]
2. Only feeding tubes that are used to deliver nutritive substances and/or hydration during the assessment period are coded in K0520B.[^7]
3. Review the medical record to determine if any of the listed nutritional approaches were performed during the look-back period; if none apply, check K0520Z.[^8]
4. When completing the Interim Payment Assessment (IPA), completion of items K0520A, K0520B, and K0520Z is required.[^9]
5. Column 2 is left blank if the resident was admitted/entered or reentered the facility more than 7 days ago.[^10]
6. A feeding tube is the presence of any type of tube that can deliver food/nutritional substances/fluids directly into the gastrointestinal system (e.g., nasogastric, gastrostomy, jejunostomy, PEG tubes).[^11]
7. Enteral feeding formulas should not be coded as a mechanically altered diet; they should only be coded as K0520D, Therapeutic Diet, when the enteral formula is altered to manage problematic health conditions.[^12]
8. Complete K0710 only if Column 2 and/or Column 3 are checked for K0520A and/or K0520B.[^13]
