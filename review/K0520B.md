## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The item's own pages state a 7-day look-back period for Column 2 (performed while NOT a resident, prior to admission/entry or reentry) and Column 3 (performed while a resident, after admission/entry or reentry); this 7-day window is the assessor's observation window and is consistent with the general convention that the standard MDS 3.0 look-back is 7 days (p. 3-3) counted as ARD plus the 6 previous calendar days (p. 2-17). Two additional, column-specific windows are stated on the item's pages: Column 1 covers days 1 through 3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B, and Column 4 covers the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending on A2400C. Column 2 is left blank if the resident was admitted/entered or reentered more than 7 days ago.

## Coding rules

1. Check all that apply for K0520; if none of the nutritional approaches apply, check K0520Z, None of the above.[^7]
2. Code K0520B for feeding tube – nasogastric or abdominal (PEG).[^6]
3. Only feeding tubes that are used to deliver nutritive substances and/or hydration during the assessment period are coded in K0520B.[^8]
4. Column 1 (On Admission): check the approach if performed during the first 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay.[^9]
5. Column 2 (While Not a Resident): check the approach if performed prior to admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the 7-day look-back period; leave Column 2 blank if the resident was admitted/entered or reentered the facility more than 7 days ago.[^10]
6. Column 3 (While a Resident): check the approach if performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the 7-day look-back period.[^1]
7. Column 4 (At Discharge): check the approach if performed within the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay.[^11]
8. When completing the Interim Payment Assessment (IPA), completion of K0520A, K0520B, and K0520Z is required.[^12]
9. A feeding tube is the presence of any type of tube that can deliver food/nutritional substances/fluids directly into the gastrointestinal system; examples include but are not limited to nasogastric tubes, gastrostomy tubes, jejunostomy tubes, and percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy (PEG) tubes.[^13]
10. Enteral feeding formulas should not be coded as a mechanically altered diet, and should only be coded as K0520D, Therapeutic Diet, when the enteral formula is altered to manage problematic health conditions.[^14]
11. Skip pattern: complete K0710 only if Column 2 and/or Column 3 are checked for K0520A and/or K0520B.[^15]

## What the chart must show

1. Review the medical record to determine if any of the listed nutritional approaches (including feeding tube) were performed during the look-back period. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^16]
   Stated as the assessment step for K0520.
2. The medical record must show the feeding tube was used to deliver nutritive substances and/or hydration during the assessment period. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^8]
