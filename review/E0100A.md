## Coding rules

1. Code based on behaviors observed and/or thoughts expressed in the last 7 days rather than the presence of a medical diagnosis; check all that apply.[^4]
2. Check E0100A (hallucinations) if hallucinations were present in the last 7 days.[^5]
3. If no hallucinations or delusions were present in the last 7 days, check E0100Z, none of the above (rather than E0100A).[^3]
4. Resident intent must not be taken into account when coding items in Section E; the section focuses on the resident's actions, not the intent of their behavior.[^6]
5. A misinterpretation of a real external sound/stimulus that the resident accepts an alternative explanation for is not coded as a hallucination or delusion; E0100Z is checked instead (Example 2: reported gunshot that was a loud knock).[^7]
6. Reported auditory and/or visual sensations occurring in the absence of any external stimulus are coded as a hallucination (E0100A checked).[^8]
