## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The Steps for Assessment for D0500 state the staff interviews are conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD. A separate symptom-recall period of the last 2 weeks is embedded in the item stem and frequency responses ("Over the last 2 weeks, did the resident have any of the following problems or behaviors?" and "Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred").

## Coding rules

1. D0500C records symptom presence and frequency for "Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much" as part of the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (PHQ-9-OV©).[^8]
2. Do not conduct the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (D0500) if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^9]
3. The staff assessment item asks whether the resident had the problem or behavior over the last 2 weeks; if the symptom is present, enter 1 (yes) in column 1, Symptom Presence, then move to column 2, Symptom Frequency, and indicate symptom frequency.[^10]
4. The Staff Assessment (D0500) is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^11]
5. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^12]
6. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^13]
7. If a longer item (such as D0500C) was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^14]
8. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^15]
9. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^16]
10. Encourage staff to report symptom frequency, even if the staff believes the symptom to be unrelated to depression.[^17]
11. Interview staff from all shifts who know the resident best; conduct the staff interview in a location that protects resident privacy.[^18]
12. Explore unclear responses, focusing the discussion on the specific symptom listed on the assessment rather than expanding into a lengthy clinical evaluation.[^19]
13. Staff responses to D0500C such as "Their back hurts when they lie down" or "They urinate a lot during the night" indicate a need for follow-up questioning with the staff member.[^20]
14. The frequency scores for D0500 Column 2 items (including D0500C) are summed into D0600, Total Severity Score, with a maximum score of 30 (3 × 10).[^21]
15. The staff interview is successfully completed if staff were able to answer the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items on the PHQ-9-OV©.[^22]
