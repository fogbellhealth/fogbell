## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The assessor's observation/administration window for the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood is the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD (p. D-13). A separate symptom-recall period is embedded in the item stem itself: staff are asked about the last 2 weeks ("Over the last 2 weeks, did the resident have any of the following problems or behaviors?", p. D-12), and the frequency codes are defined in days out of a 2-week period (p. D-15). The coexistence of these two windows is recorded in conflicts.

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. D0500B assesses the symptom "Feeling or appearing down, depressed, or hopeless" and is coded in two columns: Column 1, Symptom Presence, and Column 2, Symptom Frequency.[^8]
2. The staff assessment item asks whether, over the last 2 weeks, the resident had the problem or behavior; if the symptom is present, enter 1 (yes) in Column 1, Symptom Presence, then move to Column 2 and indicate symptom frequency.[^9]
3. Do not conduct the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (D0500, including D0500B) if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^10]
4. The Staff Assessment is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^11]
5. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^12]
6. When symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the resident interview is deemed not complete, Total Severity Score is coded "99," the Staff Assessment of Mood is not completed, and the assessor skips to D0700, Social Isolation.[^13]
7. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^14]
8. If a longer item was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^15]
9. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^16]
10. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^17]
11. Staff should be encouraged to report symptom frequency even if the staff member believes the symptom is unrelated to depression.[^18]
12. The staff interview is successfully completed if the staff members were able to answer the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items on the PHQ-9-OV©; frequency scores from D0500 Column 2 (including D0500B) are summed for D0600, Total Severity Score.[^19]

## What the chart must show

>
> - *Federal manual:* The staff interviews are to be conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
