## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The Steps for Assessment for E0500 direct the assessor to consider the behaviors identified in E0200 for the 7-day look-back period. The document does not state on this page how the 7 days are counted relative to the ARD.

## Coding rules

1. Code E0500A, E0500B, and E0500C based on all of the behavioral symptoms coded in E0200.[^4]
2. E0500 (including E0500B) is completed only when E0300 is coded 1, yes (any of E0200A, E0200B, or E0200C coded 1, 2, or 3); if E0300 is coded 0, skip to E0800.[^5]
3. Determine whether the behaviors significantly interfered with the resident's care (along with the other E0500 determinations).[^6]
4. Code 0, no, if none of the identified behavioral symptom(s) significantly interfered with the resident's care.[^2]
5. Code 1, yes, if any of the identified behavioral symptom(s) impeded the delivery of essential medical, nursing, rehabilitative or personal care, including but not limited to ADL assistance such as bathing, dressing, feeding, or toileting.[^3]
6. For E0500B, code if the impact of the resident's behavior is impeding delivery of care to such an extent that necessary or essential care cannot be received safely, completely, or in a timely way without more than a minimal accommodation, such as a simple change in care routines or environment.[^7]

## What the chart must show

1. Review of the medical record for the 7-day look-back period supporting the behaviors identified in E0200 and their impact on care. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^1]
   Stated as a Step for Assessment (record review, staff interviews across all shifts and disciplines, interviews with others, observations).
2. Documentation comparable to a baseline: subsequent assessments and documentation can be compared to a baseline to identify changes in the resident's behavior, including response to interventions. *(importance to an auditor: low)*[^8]
   Framed under Planning for Care rationale, not as required documentation.
