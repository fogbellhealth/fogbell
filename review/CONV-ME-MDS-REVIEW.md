## Coding rules

1. All residents admitted to a Nursing Facility, regardless of payment source, shall be assessed using the MDS, per CMS guidelines.[^8]
2. Admission Assessment (Comprehensive) must be completed and submitted by the 14th day of the resident's stay, where the date of admission is Day 1.[^9]
3. Annual Reassessment (Comprehensive) must be completed and submitted within 366 days of the Assessment Reference Date of the most recent comprehensive assessment.[^10]
4. Significant Change in Status Reassessment (Comprehensive) must be completed and submitted by the end of the 14th calendar day following determination that a significant change has occurred.[^11]
5. A Quarterly Assessment must be completed and submitted every 92 days.[^12]
6. Encoding: a facility must encode the data on every assessment listed in §16.2.1 within seven (7) days after the facility completes a resident's assessment.[^13]
7. Transmitting: a facility must be capable of transmitting to the State the MDS information for each resident in a format conforming to standard record layouts and data dictionaries within seven (7) days after the facility completes a resident's assessment.[^14]
8. If extraordinary conditions prevent electronic submission, a request to submit MDS information via diskette must be made to the Office of MaineCare Services a minimum of five (5) days prior to the required date of submission of the MDS assessment data.[^15]
9. MDS assessment review is a review conducted at nursing facilities by the Maine DHHS of assessments submitted under Principle 16.2 to ensure assessments accurately reflect the resident's clinical condition.[^16]
10. The assessment review error rate is the percentage of unverified Case Mix Group Records in the drawn sample; samples are drawn from Case Mix Group Records completed for residents who have MaineCare reimbursement.[^17]
11. MDS Correction Forms received in the central repository or included in the clinical record are the basis for review when completed before the day of the review and included as part of the resident's clinical record.[^18]
12. Corrections take two forms: Modification (information in the MDS central repository is inaccurate and requires correction) and Deletion (the facility determines the MDS was submitted in error and requests the inaccurate record be deleted from the database).[^19]
13. Records identified as Unverified Case Mix Group Records require facilities to submit the appropriate MDS correction form and follow CMS clinical guidelines for MDS completion; correction forms received prior to calculating the rate setting quarterly index will be used in the calculation of that index.[^20]
14. Facilities may be selected for MDS assessment review based upon, but not limited to: licensing and certification survey findings indicating the facility is not accurately assessing residents; analysis of the facility's case mix profile (including changes in frequency distribution in major categories or a change in the facility average case mix score); and prior resident assessment performance (ongoing problems with submission deadlines, error rates, high percentages of MDS corrections or deletions, and incorrect assessment dates).[^21]
15. Assessment reviews shall be conducted by staff or designated agents of the Department.[^22]
16. A record sample of twenty-four percent (24%) with a minimum of five (5) records shall be drawn from MDS assessments completed for residents who have MaineCare reimbursement.[^23]
17. At the conclusion of the on-site portion of the review, the Department's reviewers shall hold an exit conference with facility representatives and share written findings for reviewed records.[^24]
18. Sanctions are applied to the Direct Care Rate established after any applicable Guardrails are applied, for a three month period subsequent to the quality review date, and apply to all MaineCare resident days billed by the facility during the three month sanction period.[^25]
19. Upon notification of the error rates determined by the reviewers, the rate setting unit implements the sanction by issuing a rate letter with the start and end dates of the three month sanction period; at the completion of the period a rate letter is issued reinstating the total allowable inflated direct care cost per day.[^26]
20. Failure to complete MDS corrections within fourteen (14) days of a written request by staff of the Office of MaineCare Services may result in imposition of the deficiency per diem.[^27]
21. Completed MDS corrections and assessments, as defined in Principle 16.2, shall be submitted to the Department or its designee according to CMS guidelines.[^28]
22. The effective date of the rate is established by the date on the rate letter; a rate letter will be generated at least annually.[^29]

## State differences

- **ME:** Maine draws a review sample of 24% of MDS assessments completed for MaineCare-reimbursed residents, with a minimum of five (5) records.[^23]
- **ME:** Maine imposes tiered Direct Care Rate reductions (2%, 5%, 7%, 10%) for three months based on the MDS assessment review error rate (thresholds at 34%, 37%, 41% and 45%).[^4]
- **ME:** Maine requires MDS corrections to be completed within fourteen (14) days of a written request by Office of MaineCare Services staff, or the deficiency per diem may be imposed.[^27]
- **ME:** Maine requires encoding and capability of transmission of MDS data to the State within seven (7) days after a facility completes a resident's assessment, and requires a diskette-submission request at least five (5) days before the required submission date when electronic submission is impossible.[^13]
