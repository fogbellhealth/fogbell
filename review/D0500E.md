## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The assessor's observation/administration window is the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD (p. D-13). D-12) and "Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred" (p. D-15). The coexistence of the two windows is recorded in conflicts.

## Coding rules

1. D0500 (including item E, Poor appetite or overeating) is not conducted if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^8]
2. The Staff Assessment of Resident Mood is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^9]
3. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^10]
4. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the resident interview is deemed not complete, Total Severity Score is coded 99, the Staff Assessment of Mood is not completed, and the assessor skips to D0700.[^11]
5. Item E is coded in two columns: Column 1 Symptom Presence (enter 1 if symptom present) and then Column 2 Symptom Frequency.[^12]
6. Staff interviews for this item are conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
7. Interview staff from all shifts who know the resident best; conduct the staff interview in a location that protects resident privacy.[^13]
8. Encourage staff to report symptom frequency even if the staff believes the symptom to be unrelated to depression.[^14]
9. If frequency cannot be determined by staff interview because the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, talk to family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of a frequency code.[^15]
10. Ask the staff member to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^16]
11. If a longer item is separated into its component parts (e.g., "poor appetite" and "overeating"), select the highest frequency rating reported.[^17]
12. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^18]
13. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^19]
14. The Column 2 frequency score for this item is added with the other D0500 frequency items to produce D0600, Total Severity Score (maximum 30); the D0600 interview is successfully completed if staff answered the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items.[^20]
15. Certain staff responses to D0500E (e.g., "They have not wanted to eat much of anything lately"; "They have a voracious appetite, more so than last week") indicate the need for follow-up questioning with the staff member.[^21]
