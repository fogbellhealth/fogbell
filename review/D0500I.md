## Coding rules

1. D0500I asks whether the resident "States that life isn't worth living, wishes for death, or attempts to harm self," coded in Column 1 (Symptom Presence) and Column 2 (Symptom Frequency).[^8]
2. Do not conduct the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (D0500, including D0500I) if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^9]
3. The Staff Assessment (D0500) is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^10]
4. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^11]
5. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the resident interview is deemed NOT complete, D0160 is coded "99," the Staff Assessment of Mood is not completed, and the assessor skips to D0700.[^12]
6. The staff interviews for D0500 are conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
7. When Column 1 (Symptom Presence) is coded 0, enter 0 in Column 2 (Symptom Frequency); when coded 1, enter 0, 1, 2, or 3 in Column 2.[^2]
8. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^13]
9. If a longer item was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^14]
10. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^15]
11. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^16]
12. Staff should report symptom frequency even if the staff member believes the symptom to be unrelated to depression.[^17]
13. The numeric frequency score for D0500I (Column 2) is added with the other D0500 frequency items to produce D0600, Total Severity Score (maximum 30).[^18]
14. The staff interview is successfully completed if the staff members were able to answer the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items on the PHQ-9-OV©.[^19]

## What the chart must show

1. Staff interviews supporting D0500I must be conducted with staff from all shifts who know the resident best, in a location that protects resident privacy. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^20]
   Stated as an assessment step for D0500.
2. When frequency cannot be determined by staff interview because the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, family or significant other input and transfer records are used to inform the frequency code. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^21]
   Repeated as a coding tip on p. D-15.
3. Unclear staff responses to D0500I must be explored, focusing the discussion on the specific symptom listed on the assessment. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^22]
4. Example staff responses to D0500I that indicate a need for follow-up questioning with the staff member: "They say God should take them already"; "They complain that people were not meant to live like this." *(importance to an auditor: low)*[^23]
   Framed as examples, not required documentation.
