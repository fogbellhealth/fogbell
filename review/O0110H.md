## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

O0110 is a three-column item with different assessment periods per column. Column b (While a Resident) uses the last 14 days (also described on p. O-2 as "the 14-day look-back period"). Column a (On Admission) uses days 1-3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; column c (At Discharge) uses the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C (p. O-3). the look-back window is recorded as 14 for the While a Resident column; the two 3-day windows are documented here and in conflicting passages (none found).

## Coding rules

1. Code any drug or biological given by intravenous push, epidural pump, or drip through a central or peripheral port.[^10]
2. Do not code flushes to keep an IV access port patent, or IV fluids without medication.[^11]
3. Epidural, intrathecal, and baclofen pumps may be coded here.[^12]
4. Subcutaneous pumps are not coded in this item.[^13]
5. Do not include IV medications of any kind that were administered during dialysis or chemotherapy.[^14]
6. Lactated Ringers given IV is not considered a medication and should not be coded here.[^15]
7. IVs, IV medication, and blood transfusions administered during chemotherapy are not recorded under O0110H (IV Medications).[^16]
8. IVs, IV medication, and blood transfusions administered during dialysis are considered part of the dialysis procedure and are not to be coded under O0110H (IV medications).[^17]
9. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^18]
10. Do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure, such as IV medications or ventilators; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^19]
11. Column a (On Admission): check if received/performed on days 1-3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; if none in the 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^20]
12. Column b (While a Resident): check if received or performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days; if none within the last 14 days or since admission/entry or reentry, check Z, None of the above.[^21]
13. Column c (At Discharge): check if received/performed in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C; if none in the 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^8]
14. Check H2 Vasoactive medications when at least one of the IV medications was an IV vasoactive medication.[^22]
15. Check H3 Antibiotics when at least one of the IV medications was an IV antibiotic.[^23]
16. Check H4 Anticoagulation when at least one of the IV medications was an IV anticoagulant; do not include subcutaneous administration of anticoagulant medications.[^5]
17. Check H10 Other when at least one of the IV medications was not an IV vasoactive medication, IV antibiotic, or IV anticoagulant.[^24]

>
> - *Federal manual:* The item rationale describes a 14-day look-back period for the treatments, procedures, and programs in O0110.[^27]

> - *Federal manual:* Column c uses a 3-day assessment period (last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C).[^8]
>
> Status: Resolved by the document itself: the manual states the assessment period is defined separately for each column — 3 days for columns a and c, and the last 14 days for column b (p. O-3). The "14-day look-back period" phrasing on p. O-2 refers to the While a Resident column.
