## Coding rules

1. Complete O0400 only if O0390D (Respiratory Therapy) is checked.[^5]
2. Enter the number of days therapy services were provided in the last 7 days.[^1]
3. A day of therapy is defined as treatment for 15 minutes or more in the day.[^6]
4. Enter 0 if therapy was provided but for less than 15 minutes every day for the last 7 days.[^3]
5. If the total number of minutes during the last 7 days is 0, skip this item and leave it blank.[^4]
6. Days of therapy of less than 15 minutes each do not count toward the day count even if the cumulative minutes over the 7-day look-back exceed 15 (example: 50 total minutes across 5 days of 10 minutes each is coded 0 days).[^7]
