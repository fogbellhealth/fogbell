## Coding rules

1. Complete O0400 only if O0390D (Respiratory Therapy) is checked.[^5]
2. The only therapy subsection presented in O0400 is D. Respiratory Therapy, item 2 (Days).[^6]
3. Enter the number of days therapy services were provided in the last 7 days.[^1]
4. A day of therapy is defined as treatment for 15 minutes or more in the day.[^7]
5. Enter 0 if therapy was provided but for less than 15 minutes every day for the last 7 days.[^2]
6. If the total number of minutes during the last 7 days is 0, skip this item and leave it blank.[^4]
7. Minutes provided on multiple days do not aggregate across days: a total of 50 minutes over the look-back period with no single day reaching 15 minutes is coded 0 days.[^8]
