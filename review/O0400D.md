## Coding rules

1. Complete item O0400 (including D, Respiratory Therapy) only if O0390D is checked.[^5]
2. Record the number of days respiratory therapy was administered for at least 15 minutes a day in the last 7 days.[^1]
3. A day of therapy is defined as treatment for 15 minutes or more in the day.[^6]
4. Enter 0 if therapy was provided but for less than 15 minutes every day for the last 7 days.[^3]
5. If the total number of minutes during the last 7 days is 0, skip this item and leave blank.[^4]
6. Days on which therapy totaled less than 15 minutes are not counted; the example shows 10 minutes/day on 5 days (50 total minutes) coded as 0 days.[^7]
