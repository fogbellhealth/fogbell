## Coding rules

1. Complete item O0400 (including D, Respiratory Therapy) only if O0390D is checked.[^5]
2. Record the number of days respiratory therapy was administered for at least 15 minutes a day in the last 7 days.[^6]
3. A day of therapy is defined as treatment for 15 minutes or more in the day.[^7]
4. Enter 0 if therapy was provided but for less than 15 minutes every day for the last 7 days.[^3]
5. If the total number of minutes during the last 7 days is 0, skip the item and leave it blank.[^4]
6. Days on which therapy totaled less than 15 minutes are not counted, even if the cumulative minutes across the 7-day look-back period are substantial (e.g., 5 days of 10 minutes each = 0 days).[^8]
