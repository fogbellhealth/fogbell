## Coding rules

1. Add the scores for all frequency responses in D0500 Column 2, Symptom Frequency; the total score must be between 00 and 30.[^1]
2. Complete D0600 after completing the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood by adding the numeric scores across all frequency items for Staff Assessment of Mood, Symptom Frequency (D0500) Column 2.[^2]
3. The maximum score is 30 (3 × 10).[^3]
4. The interview is considered successfully completed if the staff members were able to answer the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items on the PHQ-9-OV©.[^4]
5. The software will calculate the Total Severity Score; for manual calculation instructions and examples, see Appendix E: PHQ-9-OV© Total Severity Score Scoring Rules.[^5]
