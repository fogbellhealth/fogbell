## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The assessor's observation/administration window is the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD (p. D-13). A separate symptom-recall period is embedded in the item stem and frequency codes: staff are asked about the last/past 2 weeks ("Over the last 2 weeks, did the resident have any of the following problems or behaviors?", p. D-12; "Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred," p. D-15). The coexistence of the two windows is recorded as a conflict.

## Coding rules

1. D0500 (including D0500A) is not conducted if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^8]
2. The Staff Assessment of Resident Mood is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^9]
3. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^10]
4. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the resident interview is deemed NOT complete; do not complete the Staff Assessment of Mood and skip to D0700, Social Isolation.[^11]
5. Code Column 1 Symptom Presence 0 (no) when the symptom is not present, and enter 0 in Column 2, Symptom Frequency.[^2]
6. Code Column 1 Symptom Presence 1 (yes) when the symptom is present, and enter 0, 1, 2, or 3 in Column 2, Symptom Frequency.[^3]
7. If symptom is present, enter 1 (yes) in column 1, Symptom Presence, then move to column 2, Symptom Frequency, and indicate symptom frequency.[^12]
8. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^13]
9. If a longer item was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^14]
10. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^15]
11. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^16]
12. Conduct the staff interviews during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
13. Interview staff from all shifts who know the resident best; conduct the staff interview in a location that protects resident privacy.[^17]
14. Encourage staff to report symptom frequency, even if the staff believes the symptom to be unrelated to depression.[^18]
15. If frequency cannot be determined by staff interview because the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, talk to family or significant other and review transfer records to inform the selection of a frequency code.[^19]
16. Assessors record only the presence or absence of specific clinical signs and symptoms of depressed mood; they do not make or assign a diagnosis based on the PHQ-9-OV© outcomes.[^20]
