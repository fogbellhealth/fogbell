## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The E0600 Steps for Assessment direct the assessor to consider the behaviors identified in E0200 for the 7-day look-back period. Counted per the general convention as the ARD plus the 6 previous calendar days (rai-manual-v1.20.1, p. 2-21).

## Coding rules

1. E0600 (including E0600A) is completed only when E0300 is coded 1, yes (any of E0200A, E0200B, or E0200C coded 1, 2, or 3); if E0300 is coded 0, skip to E0800.[^4]
2. Code E0600 based on the behaviors identified in item E0200; determine if those behaviors put others at significant risk of physical illness or injury.[^5]
3. For E0600A, code based on whether the behavior placed others at significant risk for physical injury. Physical injury is trauma that results in pain or other distressing physical symptoms, impaired organ function, physical disability or other adverse consequences, regardless of the need for medical, surgical, nursing, or rehabilitative intervention.[^6]
4. Resident intent must not be taken into account when coding items in Section E; the section focuses on the resident's actions, not the intent of their behavior.[^7]

## What the chart must show

1. Review of the clinical record for the 7-day look-back period supporting the behaviors identified in E0200 and their impact on others. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^1]
   Stated as a Step for Assessment for E0600, not as an explicit documentation requirement.
2. Staff interviews across all shifts and disciplines, and interviews with others who had close interactions with the resident, plus observations of the behaviors identified in E0200. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^8]
3. Subsequent assessments and documentation can be compared with a baseline to identify changes in the resident's behavior, including response to interventions. *(importance to an auditor: low)*[^9]
   Framed as Planning for Care rationale rather than a documentation requirement.
