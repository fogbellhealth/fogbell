## Look-back window

## Coding rules

1. Code E0600A based on whether the behaviors identified in E0200 put others at significant risk of physical illness or injury.[^4]
2. Code 0 (no) if none of the identified behavioral symptom(s) placed staff, visitors, or other residents at significant risk for physical injury.[^2]
3. Code 1 (yes) if any of the identified behavioral symptom(s) placed staff, visitors, or other residents at significant risk for physical injury.[^3]
4. For E0600A, code based on whether the behavior placed others at significant risk for physical injury; physical injury is trauma resulting in pain or other distressing physical symptoms, impaired organ function, physical disability or other adverse consequences, regardless of the need for medical, surgical, nursing, or rehabilitative intervention.[^5]
5. E0600 (including E0600A) is completed only when E0300 is coded 1, yes (any of E0200A, E0200B, or E0200C coded 1, 2, or 3); if E0300 is coded 0, skip to E0800.[^6]
6. Resident intent should not be taken into account when coding items in Section E; the section focuses on the resident's actions, not the intent of their behavior.[^7]

## What the chart must show

1. Review of the clinical record for the 7-day look-back period supporting the behaviors identified in E0200 and their impact on others. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^1]
   Listed as Step 1 of Steps for Assessment for E0600.
2. Staff interviews across all shifts and disciplines, and interviews with others who had close interactions with the resident, plus previous observations of the E0200 behaviors. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^8]
3. Documentation supporting a baseline for comparison on subsequent assessments to identify changes in the resident's behavior, including response to interventions. *(importance to an auditor: low)*[^9]
   Framed under Planning for Care rather than as a documentation requirement.
