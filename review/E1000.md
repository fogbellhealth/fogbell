## Coding rules

1. Complete E1000 only if E0900 (Wandering—Presence & Frequency) was coded 1, 2, or 3.[^6]
2. If E0900 is coded 0 (behavior not exhibited), skip to E1100, Change in Behavior or Other Symptoms (E1000 is not completed).[^7]
3. Determine whether wandering behaviors put the resident at significant risk of getting into potentially dangerous places and/or significantly intrude on the privacy or activities of others, based on clinical judgment for the individual resident.[^8]
4. Code E1000B as 1, yes, whether or not the other resident complains or communicates displeasure or annoyance.[^9]
5. Moving about with the use of a wheelchair meets the definition of wandering for purposes of coding E1000 impact.[^10]
