## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The Steps for Assessment direct the assessor to consider the wandering behaviors identified in E0900 for the 7-day look-back period. The manual does not state on this page how the 7 days are counted relative to the ARD.

## Coding rules

1. Complete E1000 only if E0900 (Wandering—Presence & Frequency) was coded 1, 2, or 3.[^6]
2. E1000 has two sub-items: A. Does the wandering place the resident at significant risk of getting to a potentially dangerous place (e.g., stairs, outside of the facility)? and B. Does the wandering significantly intrude on the privacy or activities of others? Each is coded 0. No or 1. Yes.[^7]
3. Base the coding on the previous review of the resident's wandering behaviors identified in E0900 for the 7-day look-back period.[^1]
4. Determine whether those behaviors put the resident at significant risk of getting into potentially dangerous places and/or whether wandering significantly intrudes on the privacy or activities of others, based on clinical judgment for the individual resident.[^8]
5. For E1000B, code 1 (yes) whether or not the other resident complains or communicates displeasure or annoyance.[^9]
6. Wandering that results in elopement from the nursing home is coded as placing the resident at significant risk of getting into a dangerous situation (E1000A = 1).[^10]
7. Moving about by propelling oneself in a wheelchair into another resident's room and blocking their bathroom door meets the definition of wandering and is coded E1000B = 1, yes.[^11]
