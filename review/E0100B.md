## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The manual specifies a 7-day look-back period for E0100; coding is based on behaviors observed and/or thoughts expressed in the last 7 days. The manual pages supplied do not state how the 7 days are counted relative to the ARD.

## Coding rules

1. Code based on behaviors observed and/or thoughts expressed in the last 7 days rather than the presence of a medical diagnosis. Check all that apply.[^4]
2. Check E0100B, delusions, if delusions were present in the last 7 days.[^5]
3. If a belief cannot be objectively shown to be false, or it is not possible to determine whether it is false, do not code it as a delusion.[^6]
4. If a resident expresses a false belief but easily accepts a reasonable alternative explanation, do not code it as a delusion; if the resident continues to insist the belief is correct despite an explanation or direct evidence to the contrary, code as a delusion.[^7]
5. Resident intent should not be taken into account when coding items in Section E; the section focuses on the resident's actions, not the intent of their behavior.[^8]
6. A false belief that is readily altered by a simple reminder (e.g., due to forgetfulness/memory loss) is not a firmly held false belief and does not meet the definition of a delusion; code E0100Z instead.[^9]
7. Misinterpretation of a real external sound or stimulus that the resident accepts an alternative explanation for is not a fixed false belief and is not a delusion (code E0100Z).[^10]

## What the chart must show

1. Review the resident's medical record for the 7-day look-back period. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^1]
   Listed as step 1 of Steps for Assessment.
2. Interview staff members and others who have had the opportunity to observe the resident in a variety of situations during the 7-day look-back period. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^11]
   Step 2 of Steps for Assessment.
3. Observe the resident during conversations and structured interviews in other assessment sections and listen for the expression of false beliefs (delusions). *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^12]
   Step 3 of Steps for Assessment.
4. Clarify potentially false beliefs: when a belief is plausible but alleged to be false, try to verify the facts to determine whether it could have happened or is likely false. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^13]
   Step 4 of Steps for Assessment.
5. When a resident expresses a clearly false belief, determine if it can be readily corrected by a simple explanation of verifiable facts or demonstration of evidence to the contrary; do not challenge the resident. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^14]
   Step 4 of Steps for Assessment.
