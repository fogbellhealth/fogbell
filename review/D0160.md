## Coding rules

1. Add scores for all frequency responses in Column 2, Symptom Frequency; the total score must be between 00 and 27. Enter 99 if unable to complete the interview (i.e., Symptom Frequency is blank for 3 or more required items).[^4]
2. After completing D0150 A–I, add the numeric scores across all frequency items in Resident Mood Interview (D0150) Column 2.[^5]
3. The maximum resident score is 27 (3 x 9).[^6]
4. If only the PHQ-2© is completed because both D0150A1 and D0150B1 are coded 9, leave D0150A2 and D0150B2 blank, end the PHQ-2©, leave D0160 blank, and skip to D0700, Social Isolation.[^3]
5. If only the PHQ-2© is completed because both D0150A2 and D0150B2 are scored 0 or 1, add the numeric scores from these two frequency items and enter the value in D0160.[^7]
6. If the PHQ-9© was completed (D0150C–I were not blank due to responses in D0150A and B) and the resident answered the frequency responses of at least 7 of the 9 items, add the numeric scores from D0150A2–D0150I2, following the instructions in Appendix E, and enter in D0160.[^8]
7. If symptom frequency in items D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the interview is deemed NOT complete: code Total Severity Score as "99," do not complete the Staff Assessment of Mood, and skip to D0700, Social Isolation.[^9]
8. Enter the total score as a two-digit number.[^10]
9. The software will calculate the Total Severity Score; for manual calculation instructions and examples, see Appendix E: PHQ-2 to 9© Total Severity Score Scoring Rules.[^11]
10. Do not add up the score while interviewing the resident; focus full attention on the interview.[^12]
