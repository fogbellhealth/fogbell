## Coding rules

1. Complete E1000 (including E1000B) only if E0900, Wandering—Presence & Frequency, was coded 1, 2, or 3.[^4]
2. If E0900 is coded 0 (behavior not exhibited), skip to E1100, Change in Behavior or Other Symptoms (E1000 is not completed).[^5]
3. Determine whether the wandering behaviors significantly intrude on the privacy or activities of others based on clinical judgment for the individual resident.[^6]
4. Code 1, yes even if the other resident does not complain or communicate displeasure or annoyance.[^7]
5. Moving about by propelling oneself in a wheelchair meets the definition of wandering; intruding into another resident's room and interfering with that resident's ability to use the bathroom is coded E1000B = 1, yes.[^8]
