## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The Steps for Assessment for E1000 direct the assessor to consider the wandering behaviors identified in E0900 for the 7-day look-back period. The document does not state on this page how the 7 days are counted relative to the ARD.

## Coding rules

1. Answer E1000 (including E1000B) only if E0900, Wandering—Presence & Frequency, was coded 1, 2, or 3.[^4]
2. Code 0, no, if the wandering does not intrude on the privacy or activity of others.[^2]
3. Code 1, yes, if the wandering intrudes on the privacy or activities of others — including violating other residents' privacy, interrupting other residents' performance of activities of daily living, or limiting engagement in or enjoyment of social or recreational activities — regardless of whether the other resident complains or communicates displeasure or annoyance.[^5]
4. Determine whether wandering significantly intrudes on the privacy or activities of others based on clinical judgment for the individual resident, considering the wandering behaviors identified in E0900 during the 7-day look-back period.[^6]
5. Locomotion in a wheelchair from place to place meets the definition of wandering; intruding into another resident's room and interfering with that resident's ability to use the bathroom is coded E1000B = 1, yes.[^7]

## What the chart must show

2. Documentation of the previous review of the resident's wandering behaviors identified in E0900 for the 7-day look-back period. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^1]
