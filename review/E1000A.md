## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The Steps for Assessment for E1000 direct the assessor to consider the wandering behaviors identified in E0900 for the 7-day look-back period. The document does not state on this page how the 7 days are counted relative to the ARD.

## Coding rules

1. Answer E1000 (including E1000A) only if E0900, Wandering—Presence & Frequency, was coded 1, 2, or 3.[^4]
2. Code 0, no, if wandering does not place the resident at significant risk.[^2]
3. Code 1, yes, if the wandering places the resident at significant risk of getting to a dangerous place or encountering a dangerous situation.[^5]
4. Determine whether the wandering behaviors put the resident at significant risk of getting into potentially dangerous places based on clinical judgment for the individual resident.[^6]
5. Wandering that results in elopement from the nursing home is coded 1, yes, for E1000A because it places the resident at significant risk of getting into a dangerous situation.[^7]
6. Locomotion in a wheelchair from place to place meets the definition of wandering for purposes of these items (E0900/E1000).[^8]

## What the chart must show

1. Review of the medical record for the 7-day look-back period supporting the wandering behaviors identified in E0900 (carried forward for coding E1000A). *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^1]
   E0900 Steps for Assessment specify reviewing the medical record and interviewing staff to determine whether wandering occurred during the 7-day look-back period (p. E-19).
2. Documentation of clinical judgment that the wandering placed the resident at significant risk of getting into a potentially dangerous place. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^6]
