## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The item's own Steps for Assessment page states a 7-day look-back period, tied to the wandering behaviors identified in E0900. Per the general convention, the ARD is the last day of the observation period and the window is ARD plus the 6 previous calendar days (rai-manual-v1.20.1, p. 2-21).

## Coding rules

1. Answer E1000 (including E1000A) only if E0900, Wandering—Presence & Frequency, was coded 1, 2, or 3.[^4]
2. If E0900 is coded 0 (behavior not exhibited), skip to Change in Behavior or Other Symptoms item (E1100) — E1000 is not completed.[^5]
3. Base the coding on the previous review of the resident's wandering behaviors identified in E0900 for the 7-day look-back period.[^1]
4. Determine whether those behaviors put the resident at significant risk of getting into potentially dangerous places, based on clinical judgment for the individual resident.[^6]
5. Wandering that results in elopement from the nursing home is coded 1, yes, as placing the resident at significant risk of getting into a dangerous situation.[^7]
6. Wandering (for purposes of E0900/E1000) is the act of moving (walking or locomotion in a wheelchair) from place to place with or without a specified course or known direction; pacing within a constrained space and traveling via a planned course to a specific place are not wandering.[^8]
