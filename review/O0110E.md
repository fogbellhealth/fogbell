## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

O0110 uses three column-specific assessment periods rather than a single window. Column b (While a Resident) — the observation window recorded here — is the last 14 days (p. O-3; item rationale on p. O-2 also calls it the "14-day look-back period"). Column a (On Admission) uses days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B, and column c (At Discharge) uses the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C (p. O-1 item table and p. O-3). The coexistence of these windows is recorded in conflicting passages (none found).

## Coding rules

1. Code cleansing of the tracheostomy and/or cannula in item O0110E1.[^6]
2. This item may be coded if the resident performs their own tracheostomy care.[^7]
3. Laryngectomy tube care is included in this item.[^8]
4. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^9]
5. Do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^10]
6. Column a (On Admission): check if the treatment was received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident on days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; if none in that 3-day period, check Z, None of the above.[^11]
7. Column b (While a Resident): check if the treatment was received or performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days; if none, check Z, None of the above.[^12]
8. Column c (At Discharge): check if the treatment was received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C; if none in that 3-day period, check Z, None of the above.[^13]
