## Coding rules

1. Complete K0710 (including K0710B) only if Column 2 and/or Column 3 are checked for K0520A and/or K0520B.[^4]
2. Add up the total amount of fluid received each day by IV and/or tube feedings only.[^5]
3. Divide the week's total fluid intake by 7 to calculate the average fluid intake per day.[^6]
4. Divide by 7 even if the resident did not receive IV fluids and/or tube feeding on each of the 7 days.[^7]
5. Code for the average number of cc per day of fluid the resident received via IV or tube feeding; record what was actually received by the resident, not what was ordered.[^8]
6. Column 2 (While a Resident) captures fluid intake performed while a resident of this facility and within the last 7 days.[^9]
7. Column 3 (During Entire 7 Days) captures fluid intake performed during the entire last 7 days, including intake received while in the hospital as well as while a resident of the nursing home.[^10]

## What the chart must show

1. Intake records from the last 7 days documenting the amount of fluid received each day by IV and/or tube feeding, used to compute the daily average. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^1]
   The manual directs the assessor to the intake records as the source for this item.
2. Coding must reflect fluid actually received by the resident, not amounts ordered; documentation should support actual administration. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^11]
