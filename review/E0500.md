## Coding rules

1. E0500 (Impact on Resident) is completed only when E0300 is coded 1, yes (any of E0200A, E0200B, or E0200C coded 1, 2, or 3).[^8]
2. If E0300 is coded 0, no (E0200A, E0200B, and E0200C all coded 0), skip E0500 and go to E0800, Rejection of Care—Presence & Frequency.[^9]
3. Code E0500A, E0500B, and E0500C based on all of the behavioral symptoms coded in E0200.[^10]
4. Determine whether the behaviors put the resident at significant risk of physical illness or injury, whether they significantly interfered with the resident's care, and/or whether they significantly interfered with the resident's participation in activities or social interactions.[^11]
5. For E0500A, code based on whether the risk for physical injury or illness is known to occur commonly under similar circumstances (i.e., with residents who exhibit similar behavior in a similar environment). Physical injury is trauma resulting in pain or other distressing physical symptoms, impaired organ function, physical disability, or other adverse consequences, regardless of the need for medical, surgical, nursing, or rehabilitative intervention.[^12]
6. For E0500B, code if the impact of the resident's behavior is impeding delivery of care to such an extent that necessary or essential care cannot be received safely, completely, or in a timely way without more than a minimal accommodation, such as a simple change in care routines or environment.[^13]
7. For E0500C, code if the impact of the resident's behavior is limiting or keeping the resident from engaging in solitary activities or hobbies, joining groups, or attending programmed activities or having positive social encounters with visitors, other residents, or staff.[^14]

## What the chart must show

1. Review of the medical record for the 7-day look-back period supporting the behaviors identified in E0200 and their impact. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^1]
   Listed as Step 1 under Steps for Assessment.
2. Staff interviews across all shifts and disciplines, and interviews with others who had close interactions with the resident, regarding the behaviors identified in E0200. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^15]
3. Documentation supporting subsequent assessment comparison to a baseline to identify changes in the resident's behavior, including response to interventions. *(importance to an auditor: low)*[^16]
   Framed under Planning for Care rather than as a coding requirement.
