## Coding rules

1. Each resident of a nursing facility, regardless of payment source, shall have a resident assessment that enables facility staff to develop a plan of care designed to assist the resident to reach their highest practicable level of physical, mental, and psychosocial functioning.[^1]
2. The Minimum Data Set (MDS) and matching Resident Assessment Protocols (RAPs) are the Department's approved Resident Assessment Instrument.[^2]
3. Each assessment must be conducted or coordinated with the appropriate participation of health professionals.[^3]
4. Certification: each individual who completes a portion of the assessment must sign and certify the accuracy of that portion of the Assessment Form.[^4]
5. Penalty for falsification: an individual who willfully and knowingly certifies (or causes another individual to certify) a material and false statement in a resident assessment is subject to civil money penalties pursuant to 42 CFR §483.20(j), in addition to possible criminal liability.[^5]
6. Use of independent assessors: if the Department determines, under a survey or otherwise, that there has been a knowing and willful certification of false statements under §67.05-6(B)(3), the Department may require, for a period it specifies, that resident assessments be conducted and certified by individuals who are independent of the facility and approved by the Department.[^6]
7. The Department may review all forms used for resident assessments at any time.[^7]
8. The Department will undertake quality reviews to ensure that assessments are completed accurately, correctly and on a timely basis.[^8]
9. The Department will undertake quality reviews to review the need for NF care for any resident.[^9]
10. All residents admitted to a nursing facility, regardless of payment source, shall be assessed using the Minimum Data Set (MDS).[^10]
11. The MDS does not meet the definition of Assessment Form in Section 67.01-17.[^11]
12. An additional case mix group is assigned when assessment data are determined to be incomplete or in error.[^12]
13. Case mix classification categories are set out in the "Principles of Reimbursement for Nursing Facilities"; refer to that document for the categories.[^13]

## State differences

- **ME:** Maine requires that each individual completing a portion of the assessment sign and certify the accuracy of that portion of the Assessment Form, and subjects willful and knowing certification of a material false statement to civil money penalties under 42 CFR §483.20(j) plus possible criminal liability.[^4]
- **ME:** Upon a determination of knowing and willful certification of false statements, the Maine Department may require, for a period it specifies, that resident assessments be conducted and certified by assessors independent of the facility and approved by the Department.[^17]
- **ME:** Maine requires MDS assessment of all residents admitted to a nursing facility regardless of payment source, and assigns an additional case mix group when assessment data are incomplete or in error.[^18]
