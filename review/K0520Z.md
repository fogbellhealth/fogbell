## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The observation window for Columns 2 and 3 is stated as the 7-day look-back period (Column 2 = performed prior to admission/entry or reentry and within the 7-day look-back; Column 3 = performed after admission/entry or reentry and within the 7-day look-back). Two additional, different windows are stated for the other columns: Column 1 covers the first 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay (assessment period is days 1 through 3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B) and Column 4 covers the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay (ending on A2400C). Because K0520Z is checked when none of K0520A–D apply in the applicable column, the window depends on the column being coded.

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. K0520 is a check-all-that-apply item; if none of the listed nutritional approaches apply, check K0520Z, None of the above.[^7]
2. During assessment, review the medical record to determine whether any listed nutritional approach was performed during the look-back period; if none apply, check K0520Z.[^8]
3. Column 1 (On Admission) reflects nutritional approaches performed during the first 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay.[^9]
4. Column 2 (While Not a Resident) reflects approaches performed prior to admission/entry or reentry and within the 7-day look-back period; leave Column 2 blank if the resident was admitted/entered or reentered the facility more than 7 days ago.[^10]
5. Column 3 (While a Resident) reflects approaches performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the 7-day look-back period.[^1]
6. Column 4 (At Discharge) reflects approaches performed within the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay.[^11]
7. When completing the Interim Payment Assessment (IPA), completion of K0520A, K0520B, and K0520Z is required.[^12]
8. The item table directs the assessor to check all of the nutritional approaches that apply, with Z. None of the above available in each of the four columns.[^13]

## What the chart must show

1. Medical record review must support that none of the listed nutritional approaches (parenteral/IV feeding, feeding tube, mechanically altered diet, therapeutic diet) were performed during the applicable look-back period before checking K0520Z. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^14]
