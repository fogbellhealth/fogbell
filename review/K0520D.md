## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

K0520 columns 2 and 3 use a 7-day look-back period (Column 2 = performed prior to admission/entry or reentry and within the last 7 days; Column 3 = performed after admission/entry or reentry and within the last 7 days). Column 1 uses a different assessment period: days 1 through 3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B. Column 4 uses the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending on A2400C. Note the item matrix on p. The 7-day figure recorded here is the assessor's observation window for the columns to which it applies.

## Coding rules

1. Check all that apply; if none of the K0520 nutritional approaches apply, check K0520Z, None of the above.[^6]
2. Code K0520D for a therapeutic diet (e.g., low salt, diabetic, low cholesterol).[^7]
3. Review the medical record to determine if any of the listed nutritional approaches were performed during the look-back period.[^8]
4. Therapeutic diets are determined by why the diet is required, not by the content of what is provided or when it is served; the diet must provide corresponding treatment addressing a particular disease or clinical condition manifesting an altered nutritional status.[^9]
5. A nutritional supplement (house or packaged) given as part of treatment for a disease or clinical condition manifesting an altered nutrition status does not by itself constitute a therapeutic diet; supplements are only coded in K0520D when administered as part of a therapeutic diet to manage problematic health conditions.[^10]
6. Food elimination diets related to food allergies (e.g., peanut allergy) can be coded as a therapeutic diet.[^11]
7. Enteral feeding formulas should only be coded as K0520D, Therapeutic Diet, when the enteral formula is altered to manage problematic health conditions (e.g., enteral formulas specific to residents with diabetes).[^12]
8. A mechanically altered diet should not automatically be considered a therapeutic diet.[^13]
9. Example: a resident with celiac disease on a gluten-free diet in the last 7 days and at discharge is coded K0520D3 and K0520D4.[^14]

## What the chart must show

1. Medical record review supporting that the listed nutritional approach was performed during the look-back period. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^8]
   Stated as the assessment step for K0520.
2. Documentation that the diet is a physician- or authorized nonphysician practitioner-prescribed intervention treating a disease or clinical condition (per the therapeutic diet definition). *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^15]
   Derived from the definition box; the manual does not separately state a documentation requirement for K0520D.
3. For a resident coded with therapeutic diet based on dysphagia/disease, the record should document the clinical condition requiring the diet (e.g., documented celiac disease requiring gluten-free diet). *(importance to an auditor: low)*[^16]
   Illustrative example, not a stated requirement.
