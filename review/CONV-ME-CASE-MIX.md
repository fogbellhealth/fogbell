## Coding rules

1. The direct care rate uses a case mix reimbursement system requiring (a) assessment of residents on the Department's approved form – MDS as specified in Principle 16.2; (b) classification of residents into groups similar in direct care staffing utilization using the case mix resident classification groups defined in Principle 22.2.1; and (c) a weighting system quantifying relative costliness to determine a facility's case mix index.[^27]
2. The Department uses only the Nursing Component of the CMS Skilled Nursing Home Patient Driven Payment Model (PDPM) to determine the Case Mix Index for each resident.[^28]
3. The Nursing Component of the PDPM is the case mix classification used to assign a resident-specific direct care rate; it is derived from items on the MDS 3.0 and classifies residents into one of 25 groups based on the use of specialized services, the presence of certain clinical conditions and medical diagnoses, the use of restorative nursing services, and the patient's functional score.[^29]
4. One additional resident classification group is used when residents cannot be classified into one of the twenty-five clinical classification groups, creating a total of twenty-six groups as listed in the Nursing Component PDPM Groups table in 22.2.1 Maine Weights.[^30]
5. The MDS provides the basis for resident classification into one of twenty-five case mix classification groups; an additional unclassified group is assigned when assessment data are determined to be incomplete or in error.[^31]
6. Per CMS guidelines, all residents admitted to a Nursing Facility, regardless of payment source, shall be assessed using the MDS.[^32]
7. Each nursing component case mix classification group has a specific Maine weight that is multiplied by the HPD to establish the Direct Care Rate.[^33]
8. The Daily Rate is computed as (Direct Care Rate × Nursing Component PDPM Group Weight) + Routine Care Rate + Capital Cost Rate + Bariatric Add-on and/or Ventilator Add-on (if applicable).[^34]
9. The Daily Rate is comprised of three components: Direct Care Rate, Routine Care Rate, and Capital Cost Rate; plus a Bariatric Care Add-on for eligible Members.[^35]
10. Maine weights were determined by totaling all Maine nursing facilities' resident days in calendar years 2022 and 2023 for each PDPM nursing group, multiplying days by the 2023 CMS PDPM Nursing weight to get case mix weighted resident days, dividing by total resident days to get the Maine average case mix index (CMI = 1.356 using CMS weights), then dividing each nursing group's CMS CMI by the Maine average CMI to determine the standardized weight, so that the average Maine CMI is 1.0.[^36]
11. Case mix weights will be adjusted at rebasing.[^37]
12. Case Mix Weight is defined as a relative evaluation of the nursing resources used in the care of a given class of residents.[^38]
13. Reimbursement is based on OBRA assessment outcomes; except for the admission assessment, the active OBRA assessment sets the payment from the Assessment Reference Date (ARD) until the day before the ARD on the next required OBRA assessment, and the admission assessment sets payment from the admission date until the next required OBRA assessment.[^39]
14. The Direct Care Rate to which the group weight is applied is $244.86 (table: $244.87) for CY 2025 and $232.01 for CY 2026 and forward.[^40]
15. For nursing home conversions, the case mix index will be determined as stated in Principles 16.2, 22.3.1, 22.3.2, and 22.3.3.2.[^41]
16. A record whose classification does not accurately represent the resident's condition and results in inaccurate classification into a case mix group that increases the case mix weight assigned to the resident is an 'Unverified Case Mix Group Record'; facilities must submit the appropriate MDS correction form and follow CMS clinical guidelines for MDS completion. Correction forms received prior to calculating the rate setting quarterly index will be used in the calculation of that index.[^42]

## State differences

- **ME:** Maine uses only the Nursing Component of the CMS PDPM (not the full PDPM) to determine each resident's Case Mix Index for MaineCare nursing facility payment.[^28]
- **ME:** Maine applies its own standardized case mix weights (derived from 2023 CMS PDPM Nursing weights divided by the Maine average CMI of 1.356) rather than CMS weights, so the average Maine CMI equals 1.0.[^50]
- **ME:** Maine assigns a 26th 'Default' group (weight 0.47, code ZZZZ6) for residents who cannot be classified into one of the 25 clinical groups, including when assessment data are incomplete or in error.[^51]
