## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

The observation window differs by column. Column b (While a Resident) uses the last 14 days (and/or since admission/entry or reentry), which is the item's stated look-back period ("14-day look-back period," p. O-2). Column a (On Admission) uses days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B, and Column c (At Discharge) uses the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C (p. O-3). the look-back window is recorded as 14 for the While a Resident column; the 3-day column windows are recorded as a conflict/coexistence below.

## Coding rules

1. Code O0110Z1 (None of the above) if none of the treatments, procedures, or programs listed in O0110 were received or performed by the resident.[^5]
2. For Column a (On Admission), check Z, None of the above, if no treatments, procedures, or programs were received or performed in the 3-day assessment period (days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B).[^6]
3. For Column b (While a Resident), check Z, None of the above, if no treatments, procedures or programs were received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident within the last 14 days or since admission/entry or reentry.[^3]
4. For Column c (At Discharge), check Z, None of the above, if no treatments, procedures or programs were received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C.[^7]
5. When determining whether any O0110 treatment applies (and therefore whether Z1 is checked), treatments, programs and procedures the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff may be coded; services provided solely in conjunction with a surgical or diagnostic procedure (e.g., IV medications or ventilators), including routine pre- and post-operative procedures, are not coded.[^8]
6. O0110 is completed by checking all of the treatments, procedures, and programs that were performed within the assessment period defined for each column (a. On Admission, b. While a Resident, c. At Discharge); Z1 has a checkbox in all three columns.[^9]

## ⚠️ Conflicts — please read carefully

The manual (or two documents) say different things here. Both sides are shown; nothing has been dropped.

> **Coexisting observation windows for O0110 (14 days vs. 3-day column windows)**
>
> - *Federal manual:* Column b (While a Resident) uses a 14-day look-back: treatments performed after admission/entry or reentry and within the last 14 days; Z is checked if none occurred in that window.[^1]
> - *Federal manual:* Columns a and c use 3-day assessment periods (days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C); Z is checked if none occurred in the 3-day assessment period.[^11]
> - *Federal manual:* The Item Rationale describes the item as having a "14-day look-back period."[^12]
>
> Status: Resolved by the document itself: the manual states the assessment period is "defined for each column" (p. O-3) — 3 days for columns a and c, 14 days (or since admission/entry/reentry) for column b. The 14-day figure in the Item Rationale corresponds to the While a Resident column.
