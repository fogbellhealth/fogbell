## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

O0110 uses three different assessment periods, one per column. Column b (While a Resident) uses the last 14 days (or since admission/entry or reentry), which is the general observation window recorded here and is also referred to in the Item Rationale as "the 14-day look-back period" (p. O-2). Column a (On Admission) uses days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B, and column c (At Discharge) uses the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C (p. O-3). See conflicting passages (none found) for the coexisting windows.

## Coding rules

1. Code O0110O1 when the resident has IV access, i.e., a catheter inserted into a vein for clinical reasons including long-term medication administration, large volumes of blood or fluid, frequent access for blood samples, intravenous fluid administration, TPN, or measurement of central venous pressure.[^2]
2. An arteriovenous (AV) fistula does not meet the definition of IV Access and is not coded in O0110O1.[^6]
3. Check O0110O2 (Peripheral) when IV access was peripheral (catheter placed in a peripheral vein) and remains peripheral.[^3]
4. Check O0110O3 (Midline) when IV access was midline access (catheter inserted into the antecubital or other upper arm vein that does not reach a central vein such as the superior vena cava).[^4]
5. Check O0110O4 (Central) when IV access was centrally located (e.g., PICC, tunneled, port).[^5]
6. Column a (On Admission): check treatments, procedures, and programs received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident on days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B.[^7]
7. Column b (While a Resident): check treatments, procedures, and programs the resident received or performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days.[^1]
8. Column c (At Discharge): check treatments, procedures, and programs received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C.[^8]
9. If no treatments, procedures, or programs (including IV access) were received or performed in the applicable assessment period, check Z, None of the above (O0110Z1).[^9]
10. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^10]
11. Do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^11]

## What the chart must show

1. Review the resident's medical record to determine whether or not the resident received or performed any of the treatments, procedures, or programs (including IV access) within the assessment period defined for each column. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^12]
