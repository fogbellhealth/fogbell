## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The manual instructs that the staff interviews for D0500 be conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD. A separate symptom-recall period is embedded in the item stem and frequency coding: staff report on the last/past 2 weeks ("Over the last 2 weeks, did the resident have any of the following problems or behaviors?" p. D-12; "Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred." p. D-15). Frequency codes are defined in day counts spanning up to 14 days.

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. D0500D captures the symptom "Feeling tired or having little energy" and is scored in two columns: 1. Symptom Presence and 2. Symptom Frequency.[^8]
2. Do not conduct the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (D0500) if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^9]
3. The staff assessment asks whether, over the last 2 weeks, the resident had the problem or behavior; if the symptom is present, enter 1 (yes) in column 1 and then indicate symptom frequency in column 2.[^10]
4. Conduct the staff interviews during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
5. Interview staff from all shifts who know the resident best; conduct the staff interview in a location that protects resident privacy.[^11]
6. Encourage staff to report symptom frequency even if the staff believes the symptom to be unrelated to depression.[^12]
7. If frequency cannot be determined by staff interview because the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, talk to family or significant other and review transfer records to inform the selection of a frequency code.[^13]
8. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form.[^14]
9. If a longer item was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^15]
10. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^16]
11. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^17]
12. Complete the Staff Assessment (D0500) only when D0100 is coded 0, No; the assessor does not complete the Staff Assessment based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^18]
13. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^19]
14. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the resident interview is deemed NOT complete; do not complete the Staff Assessment of Mood, and skip to D0700, Social Isolation.[^20]
