## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

O0110 is a three-column item, each column with its own stated assessment period. The general observation/look-back window (Column b, While a Resident) is the last 14 days since admission/entry or reentry; the manual also refers to this as "the 14-day look-back period" (p. O-2). Two additional, shorter stated windows apply to the other columns: Column a (On Admission) = days 1-3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B, and Column c (At Discharge) = the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C (p. O-1, p. O-3).

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. Column a (On Admission): check oxygen therapy received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident on days 1-3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; if no treatments were received or performed in the 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^10]
2. Column b (While a Resident): check oxygen therapy the resident received or performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days; if none within the last 14 days or since admission/entry or reentry, check Z, None of the above.[^11]
3. Column c (At Discharge): check oxygen therapy received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C; if none in the 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^12]
4. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^13]
5. Do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^14]
6. Code continuous or intermittent oxygen administered via mask, cannula, etc., delivered to a resident to relieve hypoxia in O0110C1.[^5]
7. Code oxygen used in Bi-level Positive Airway Pressure/Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (BiPAP/CPAP) in O0110C1.[^15]
8. Do not code hyperbaric oxygen for wound therapy in O0110C1.[^16]
9. O0110C1 may be coded if the resident places or removes their own oxygen mask or cannula.[^17]
10. Check O0110C2 (Continuous) if oxygen therapy was continuously delivered for 14 hours or greater per day.[^6]
11. Check O0110C3 (Intermittent) if oxygen therapy was intermittent, i.e., not delivered continuously for at least 14 hours per day.[^7]
12. Check O0110C4 (High-concentration) if oxygen therapy was provided via a high-concentration delivery system, defined as one that delivers oxygen at a concentration exceeding an FiO2 of 40% (i.e., exceeding that of simple low-flow nasal cannula at a flow rate of 4 liters per minute).[^18]
13. A high-concentration delivery system can include either high- or low-flow systems (e.g., simple face masks, partial and nonrebreather masks, face tents, venturi masks, aerosol masks, and high-flow cannula or masks).[^19]
14. High-concentration devices may also include invasive mechanical ventilators, non-invasive mechanical ventilators, or trach masks, if the delivered FiO2 of these systems exceeds 40%.[^20]
15. Oxygen-conserving nasal cannula systems with reservoirs (e.g., mustache, pendant) should be included as high-concentration only if they are used to deliver an FiO2 of greater than 40%.[^21]
16. On the item set, the sub-elements C2 (Continuous), C3 (Intermittent), and C4 (High-concentration) have check boxes only in Column a (On Admission) and Column c (At Discharge); C1 (Oxygen therapy) has check boxes in all three columns (a, b, c).[^22]

## ⚠️ Conflicts — please read carefully

The manual (or two documents) say different things here. Both sides are shown; nothing has been dropped.

> **Coexistence of multiple assessment/look-back windows for O0110C**
>
> - *RAI Manual v1.20.1 (Oct 2025):* Column b (While a Resident) uses a 14-day window; the Item Rationale calls this the 14-day look-back period.[^24]
> - *RAI Manual v1.20.1 (Oct 2025):* Column a uses a 3-day window (days 1-3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B) and Column c uses a 3-day window (last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C).[^25]
>
> Status: Resolved by the document: the assessment period is defined separately for each column (p. O-3, "within the assessment period defined for each column"); the 14-day window applies to Column b, and 3-day windows apply to Columns a and c.
