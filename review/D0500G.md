## Look-back window

**7 days**[^1]

The staff interview for D0500 is conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD (p. D-13). Within the interview itself, staff are asked about symptoms "Over the last 2 weeks" and frequency is coded on a 14-day scale (0-1, 2-6, 7-11, 12-14 days) (p. D-12, p. D-15). The 2-week window is the symptom-recall period inside the interview question, not the assessor observation window.

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. D0500G is the item "Trouble concentrating on things, such as reading the newspaper or watching television" within the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (PHQ-9-OV©); both Column 1 (Symptom Presence) and Column 2 (Symptom Frequency) are scored.[^8]
2. Do not conduct the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood (D0500) if the Resident Mood Interview (D0150–D0160) was completed.[^9]
3. The staff assessment asks, over the last 2 weeks, whether the resident had the problem or behavior; if the symptom is present, enter 1 (yes) in column 1, Symptom Presence, then move to column 2, Symptom Frequency, and indicate symptom frequency.[^10]
4. Conduct the staff interviews during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
5. Ask the staff member being interviewed to select how often over the past 2 weeks the symptom occurred, using the descriptive and/or numeric categories on the form to select a frequency response.[^11]
6. If a longer item was separated into its component parts, select the highest frequency rating that is reported.[^12]
7. If the staff member has difficulty selecting between two frequency responses, code for the higher frequency.[^13]
8. If the resident has been in the facility for less than 2 weeks, also talk to the family or significant other and review transfer records to inform selection of the frequency code.[^14]
9. Encourage staff to report symptom frequency, even if the staff believes the symptom to be unrelated to depression.[^15]
10. Interview staff from all shifts who know the resident best, in a location that protects resident privacy.[^16]
11. Explore unclear responses, focusing the discussion on the specific symptom listed on the assessment rather than expanding into a lengthy clinical evaluation. For D0500G, staff responses such as "They say there's nothing good on TV," "They never watch TV," or "They can't see to read a newspaper" indicate a need for follow-up questioning.[^17]
12. Do not complete the Staff Assessment of Resident Mood items (D0500) if the resident interview should have been conducted but was not done, or if the assessment being completed is a stand-alone Part A PPS Discharge assessment.[^18]
13. The Staff Assessment is completed only when D0100 is coded 0, No; it is not completed based on resident performance during the Resident Mood Interview.[^19]
14. If symptom frequency in D0150A2 through D0150I2 is blank or dashed for 3 or more items, the interview is deemed NOT complete, Total Severity Score is coded "99," the Staff Assessment of Mood is not completed, and the assessor skips to D0700, Social Isolation.[^20]
15. D0500G's frequency score contributes to D0600, Total Severity Score, which is the sum of all frequency responses in D0500 Column 2 and must be between 00 and 30.[^21]
16. The staff interview is successfully completed if the staff members were able to answer the frequency responses of at least 8 out of 10 items on the PHQ-9-OV©.[^22]

## ⚠️ Conflicts — please read carefully

> **Two time windows apply to D0500G: a 7-day assessor look-back period and a 2-week symptom-recall period inside the staff interview questions.**
>
> - *Federal manual:* Staff interviews are conducted during the 7-day look-back period based on the ARD.[^1]
> - *Federal manual:* Staff are asked whether the resident had the problem or behavior over the last 2 weeks, and frequency is coded on a 14-day scale.[^26]
>
> Status: unresolved — surface to user. The manual states both without reconciling them; the 7-day window governs when the interview is conducted, while the interview question itself asks about the past 2 weeks.
