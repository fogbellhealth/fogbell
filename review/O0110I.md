## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

O0110 uses column-specific assessment periods rather than a single window: Column a. On Admission = days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; Column b. While a Resident = the last 14 days (after admission/entry or reentry); Column c. At Discharge = the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C. The 14-day period recorded in the look-back window is the 'While a Resident' observation window; the two 3-day windows are also stated on p. O-1 and p. O-3.

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. Code transfusions of blood or any blood products (e.g., platelets, synthetic blood products) that are administered directly into the bloodstream in this item.[^5]
2. Do not include transfusions that were administered during dialysis or chemotherapy.[^6]
3. Blood transfusions administered during chemotherapy are not recorded under O0110I (Transfusions).[^7]
4. Blood transfusions administered during dialysis are considered part of the dialysis procedure and are not to be coded under O0110I (transfusions).[^8]
5. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^9]
6. Do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^10]
7. Transfusions (O0110I1) may be checked in all three columns: a. On Admission, b. While a Resident, and c. At Discharge.[^11]
8. If no treatments, procedures, or programs were received or performed in the applicable assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^12]
