# Fogbell review packet

This folder holds one page per MDS item that Fogbell has pulled out of the official RAI Manual, rewritten in plain English so it can be checked. Each page lists the item's look-back window, its coding levels, the coding rules, what the chart is supposed to show, and any places where the manual seems to say two different things. Every statement carries a small source number, and the sources at the bottom of the page give the document, the printed page (for example "p. D-13"), and the exact wording, so you can pull the manual and check for yourself. Nothing on these pages has been checked by a person yet; the software is careful to say "not found" rather than guess, but it can still be wrong. `QUESTIONS.md` lists the questions we most need your answer on.

Mark them up however is easiest for you: pen on a printout, a reply email, a voice memo, or a phone call. For each rule, the three boxes are "correct", "wrong (how?)", and "auditors apply it differently (how?)". The third one matters most: what the state's case-mix reviewers actually look for in a chart outranks what the manual says, and your corrections become the authoritative layer of Fogbell's rulebook. Anything you add is recorded with your name as the source, above the manual, and is never overwritten by the software.
