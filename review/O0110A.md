## Coding rules

1. Code any type of chemotherapy agent administered as an antineoplastic given by any route in item O0110A1.[^2]
2. Each medication must be evaluated to determine its reason for use before coding it as chemotherapy; only medications actually used for cancer treatment are coded here (e.g., megestrol acetate given only for appetite stimulation is not coded as chemotherapy).[^7]
3. Hormonal and other agents administered to prevent the recurrence or slow the growth of cancer are not coded as chemotherapy for MDS purposes (e.g., tamoxifen given to decrease risk of recurrence).[^8]
4. IVs, IV medication, and blood transfusions administered during chemotherapy are not recorded under K0520A (Parenteral/IV), O0110H (IV Medications), or O0110I (Transfusions).[^9]
5. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^10]
6. Do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure, such as IV medications or ventilators; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^11]
7. Column a (On Admission): check all treatments, procedures, and programs received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident on days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; if none in the 3-day assessment period, check Z, None of the above.[^12]
8. Column b (While a Resident): check all treatments, procedures, and programs that the resident received or performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days; if none, check Z, None of the above.[^13]
9. Column c (At Discharge): check all treatments, procedures, and programs received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C; if none, check Z, None of the above.[^14]
10. Check O0110A2 (IV) when chemotherapy was administered intravenously.[^15]
11. Check O0110A3 (Oral) when chemotherapy was administered orally (pills, capsules, liquids swallowed), including chemotherapy administered through a feeding tube/PEG (enterally).[^4]
12. Check O0110A10 (Other) when chemotherapy was given by a route other than intravenous or oral (e.g., intramuscular, intraventricular/intrathecal, intraperitoneal, topical).[^5]
13. Per the item table layout, A1 Chemotherapy has check boxes for all three columns (a. On Admission, b. While a Resident, c. At Discharge), while the route sub-items A2 (IV), A3 (Oral), and A10 (Other) have check boxes only in column a (On Admission) and column c (At Discharge).[^16]
