## Look-back window

**14 days**[^1]

The observation window differs by column. Column b (While a Resident) uses a 14-day look-back ("within the last 14 days" after admission/entry or reentry); the item rationale also refers to "the 14-day look-back period" (p. O-2). Column a (On Admission) uses days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B, and column c (At Discharge) uses the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C (p. O-1, p. O-3). the look-back window is recorded as 14 for the While a Resident column; the two 3-day column windows are recorded here and as a conflict.

> **Note:** the manual states more than one time period for this item. See the *Conflicts* section below.

## Coding rules

1. Facilities may code treatments, programs and procedures that the resident performed themselves independently or after set-up by facility staff.[^38]
2. Do not code services that were provided solely in conjunction with a surgical procedure or diagnostic procedure, such as IV medications or ventilators; surgical procedures include routine pre- and post-operative procedures.[^39]
3. Review the resident's medical record to determine whether or not the resident received or performed any of the treatments, procedures, or programs within the assessment period defined for each column.[^40]
4. Column a (On Admission): check all treatments, procedures, and programs received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident on days 1–3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B; if none, check Z, None of the above.[^41]
5. Column b (While a Resident): check all treatments, procedures, and programs the resident received or performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the last 14 days; if none, check Z, None of the above.[^42]
6. Column c (At Discharge): check all treatments, procedures, and programs received by, performed on, or participated in by the resident in the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending with A2400C; if none, check Z, None of the above.[^43]
7. O0110A1 Chemotherapy: each medication must be evaluated to determine its reason for use before coding; only medications actually used for cancer treatment are coded (e.g., megestrol acetate given only for appetite stimulation is not coded as chemotherapy).[^44]
8. O0110A1: hormonal and other agents administered to prevent the recurrence or slow the growth of cancer are not coded as chemotherapy for MDS purposes.[^45]
9. IVs, IV medication, and blood transfusions administered during chemotherapy are not recorded under K0520A (Parenteral/IV), O0110H (IV Medications), or O0110I (Transfusions).[^46]
10. O0110C1 Oxygen therapy: do not code hyperbaric oxygen for wound therapy; the item may be coded if the resident places or removes their own oxygen mask or cannula.[^47]
11. O0110C4: a high-concentration delivery system can include high- or low-flow systems, and may include invasive or non-invasive mechanical ventilators or trach masks if the delivered FiO2 exceeds 40%; oxygen-conserving nasal cannula systems with reservoirs are included only if used to deliver an FiO2 greater than 40%.[^48]
12. O0110D1 Suctioning may be coded if the resident performs their own tracheal and/or nasopharyngeal suctioning.[^49]
13. O0110F1: a resident who has been weaned off a respirator or ventilator in the last 14 days, or who is currently being weaned off a respirator or ventilator, should also be coded here.[^50]
14. O0110F1: do not code this item when the ventilator or respirator is used only as a substitute for BiPAP or CPAP.[^51]
15. O0110G1: if a ventilator or respirator is being used as a substitute for BiPAP/CPAP, code here; the item may be coded if the resident places or removes their own BiPAP/CPAP mask/device.[^52]
16. O0110H1: do not code flushes to keep an IV access port patent, or IV fluids without medication; subcutaneous pumps are not coded; epidural, intrathecal, and baclofen pumps may be coded here.[^53]
17. O0110H1: do not include IV medications of any kind that were administered during dialysis or chemotherapy; Lactated Ringers given IV is not considered a medication and should not be coded here.[^54]
18. O0110I1: do not include transfusions that were administered during dialysis or chemotherapy.[^55]
19. O0110J1: IVs, IV medication, and blood transfusions administered during dialysis are considered part of the dialysis procedure and are not to be coded under K0520A, O0110H, or O0110I; the item may be coded if the resident performs their own dialysis.[^56]
20. O0110K1: the hospice must be licensed by the state as a hospice provider and/or certified under the Medicare program as a hospice provider.[^57]
21. O0110M1: do not code if the resident only has a history of infectious disease (e.g., s/p MRSA or s/p C-Diff with no active symptoms), and do not code if the precautions are standard precautions.[^58]
22. O0110M1: code for "single room isolation" only when all four conditions are met — (1) active infection with highly transmissible or epidemiologically significant pathogens acquired by physical contact or airborne or droplet transmission; (2) transmission-based precautions over and above standard precautions in effect; (3) the resident is in a room alone because of active infection and cannot have a roommate; and (4) the resident must remain in their room with all services brought to them.[^59]
23. O0110M1: if a facility transports a resident who meets the criteria for single room isolation to another healthcare setting for medically needed services the facility cannot provide, they may still code O0110M for single room isolation since it is still being maintained while the resident is in the facility.[^60]
24. O0110O1: an arteriovenous (AV) fistula does not meet the definition of IV Access for O0110O1.[^61]

## What the chart must show

1. The resident's medical record must support that the treatment, procedure, or program was received or performed within the assessment period defined for each column. *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^40]
   Stated as the single Step for Assessment for this item.
2. For O0110A1 (Chemotherapy), documentation of each medication's reason for use is needed to establish that it was administered for cancer treatment rather than another purpose (e.g., appetite stimulation). *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^62]
3. For O0110M1 (Isolation), evidence that transmission-based precautions were in effect and that the resident was alone in a room and remained in the room with all services brought to them (all four listed conditions). *(importance to an auditor: high)*[^63]
   All four conditions on p. O-8 must be met to code single room isolation.
