The assessor's observation window for the resident-status columns is the 7-day look-back period (Column 2: performed prior to admission/entry or reentry and within the 7-day look-back period; Column 3: performed after admission/entry or reentry and within the 7-day look-back period). Two other, different windows are stated for the SNF PPS Stay columns: Column 1 is days 1 through 3 of the SNF PPS Stay starting with A2400B, and Column 4 is the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay ending on A2400C. ## Coding rules

1. Check all nutritional approaches that apply; if none apply, check K0520Z, None of the above.[^3]
2. Code K0520C for a mechanically altered diet, i.e., a diet that requires a change in texture of food or liquids (e.g., pureed food, thickened liquids).[^2]
3. A mechanically altered diet is a diet specifically prepared to alter the texture or consistency of food to facilitate oral intake (e.g., soft solids, puréed foods, ground meat, thickened liquids); it should not automatically be considered a therapeutic diet.[^8]
4. Do not capture a trial of a mechanically altered diet (e.g., pureed food, thickened liquids) during the observation period in K0520C.[^9]
5. Enteral feeding formulas should not be coded as a mechanically altered diet.[^10]
6. Column 1: check all nutritional approaches performed during the first 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay.[^11]
7. Column 2: check all nutritional approaches performed prior to admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the 7-day look-back period; leave Column 2 blank if the resident was admitted/entered or reentered the facility more than 7 days ago.[^12]
8. Column 3: check all nutritional approaches performed after admission/entry or reentry to the facility and within the 7-day look-back period.[^1]
9. Column 4: check all nutritional approaches performed within the last 3 days of the SNF PPS Stay.[^13]
10. A documented mechanical soft diet with honey-thick liquids required in the last 7 days and continuing at discharge is coded in K0520C for the While a Resident and At Discharge columns (example: K0520C3 and K0520C4 checked).[^14]
11. A resident who was on a mechanical soft diet only during the acute hospital stay and was upgraded to a regular texture diet prior to hospital discharge, and who had a regular diet for the entire nursing home stay, is coded K0520Z (none of the above) rather than K0520C for the While a Resident and At Discharge columns.[^15]

## What the chart must show

1. Review the medical record to determine whether any of the listed nutritional approaches (including mechanically altered diet) were performed during the look-back period. *(importance to an auditor: medium)*[^16]
