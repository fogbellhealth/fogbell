§1 Coding levels
0
Never or 1 day
Symptom not observed, or observed on a single day
1
Several days
2–6 days in the look-back
2
Half or more of the days
7–11 days in the look-back
3
Nearly every day
12–14 days in the look-back
§2 Coding rules
2.1
Frequency is counted in days on which the symptom was observed, not in number of observations. Two notes on the same day count once. [S1]
2.2
The staff assessment is completed only when the resident interview (D0200) could not be conducted. [S1]
2.3
Observations dated before the window opens or after the ARD are not counted toward frequency. [S1, S2]
§3 Documentation criteria
3.1
Each counted day needs a dated, signed staff observation describing the mood symptom in the resident's words or observable behavior.
3.2
The observation may come from any discipline across all shifts; the assessor consolidates them. [S1]
3.3
Auditors sampled under MaineCare review compare the coded frequency against the count of dated observations in the record. [S3]
§4 Conflicts between sources
Length of the look-back window
Unresolved · Fogbell applies 7 days until reviewed

The D0500 frequency scale spans 14 days (“12–14 days” at code 3), implying a 14-day observation period.

D0500 Staff assessment of mood
Open full rule →
Plain-English rendering · as Fogbell applies it
Count the number of days in the 7-day look-back on which staff documented the mood symptom. Two notes on one day count once. Code 1 for 2–6 days, 2 for 7–11, 3 for 12–14. Notes dated before the window opened do not count.
